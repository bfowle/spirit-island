# Spirit Island Mastery Guide — Research Sources Dump

Compiled 2026-04-19. Primary reference material for chapter authors. The structure and terminology here — especially Rei's — is canonical for this project.

## Part A: latentoctopus.github.io Spirit Island Hub

### Overview

The latentoctopus.github.io hub provides strategic guides for 10 spirits with detailed opening variants, conceptual frameworks, glossary entries, and Q&A. All guides are authored by a Level 6 difficulty player and marked with confidence ratings (red/tentative, yellow/somewhat confident, green/well-tested).

### Glossary (canonical notation for this book)

#### Abbreviations & Notation
- **Gn**: the n-th growth option, counted left to right starting at 1
- **nE**: energy per turn (e.g., 2E = 2 energy/turn)
- **nCP**: card plays per turn (e.g., 3CP = 3 card plays)
- **x/y**: combined energy + card plays (e.g., 3/2 = 3 energy, 2 card plays)
- **CE**: Cumulative Energy, total energy gained via growth throughout the game

#### Key Strategic Terms
- **Reclaim cycle**: the turns between a Reclaim (included) and the following Reclaim (excluded)
- **Reclaim loop**: a reclaim cycle lasting just one turn (reclaiming every turn)

#### Opening Strategy Classifications (Presence Placement)
- **Full top/bottom track**: focuses on emptying one track with minimal presence from the other
- **Top/bottom track**: mostly takes from one track, occasionally from the other
- **Hybrid**: presence taken from both tracks more or less equally

#### Opening Strategy Classifications (Power Card Gains)
- **Minor**: no Major powers or only late-game gains
- **Major**: acquiring strong Major powers early
- **Mixed**: balanced approach between major and minor acquisition

#### Confidence Rating System
- **Red**: tentative / theorycrafting (limited confidence)
- **Yellow**: somewhat confident with limited play-testing
- **Green**: well-tested with consistent success

### Q&A — key points

- Resources collected are links to game discussion and educational materials; excludes buying/selling.
- Contributions via GitHub PR or direct contact.
- Guides represent a level-6 player's directional analysis, not absolute truth.
- Target audience: medium-to-high difficulty players.

### Spirit Guides catalog

#### 1. Downpour Drenches the World

**Concept guide**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/downpour-concepts/
- Core capabilities: Explorer control, Isolation, stalling, building damage, blight removal, Energy support.
- Opening principle: **strategic complementarity** — draft Minor powers that *fill gaps* in the spirit's toolkit rather than duplicate strengths.
- Gap areas: Explorer Removal (vs. Russia); Defend Effects that don't suppress counterattacks.

**Opening 1 (Full)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/downpour-opening1/
- T1: G3 bottom + Minor gain; Gift of Abundance recommended for scaling.
- T2–3: G2 bottom + reclaim cycle; accumulate Minor cards and Water elements.
- Prioritize repeated Pour Down activations over Majors. "2 CP coupled with your Innates are usually enough to deal with a variety of issues."
- Presence starts bottom, shifts upward later.

#### 2. Ember-Eyed Behemoth

**Hybrid Opening**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/behemoth-opening1/
- T1: G3 bottom + Minor gain; play to unlock Smash/Stomp/Flatten.
- T2: G2 top + play two cards.
- T3: G4 + reclaim + empower Incarna + play two cards.
- Keep a fire-plant element (e.g., Grasping Roots) in hand T1 to unlock level 2 Innate next turn.
- Surging Lahar preferred over Blazing Intimidation when early Badlands targets exist.
- Presence arc: 10 on T1 declining to 5 by T6.

**Early Major Opening**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/behemoth-opening2/
- T1: G2 top + Major gain + forget Flash-Fires + Surging Lahar.
- T2: G3 top + play Major or Asphyxiating Smoke.
- T3: G2 bottom + Flame's Fury + others.
- Targets tough adversaries where early Major replacement pays off.

#### 3. Grinning Trickster Stirs Up Trouble

**Opening 1 (Hybrid)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/trickster-opening1/
- Rapid growth to unlock "Let's See What Happens" level 2.
- T1 G3; T2–3 G2.
- Seek 0-cost Moon/Air Minors.
- Trades early card economy for energy efficiency + mid-game flexibility.
- 3 cards/turn only by T4, 4 by T6.

**Opening 2 (Bottom Track)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/trickster-opening2/
- T1: G3; G2 bottom. Moon/Air 0-cost Minor + Overenthusiastic Arson + Impersonate Authority.
- 10 presence T1, declining.
- 3 CP on T2, 4 CP on T5.
- Heavy energy constraint; keep total hand cost ≤ 3.

**Opening 3 (Hybrid Majors)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/trickster-opening3/
- T1: G3; G2 top.
- T2: conditional — Air? Impersonate Authority + Overenthusiastic Arson. Else Unexpected Tigers.
- T3: reclaim + two cards, gather 2 Moon + 1 Fire + 2 Air to unlock level 2 Innate.
- 7 presence + 3 CP by T5.

#### 4. Heart of the Wildfire

**Concepts**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/wildfire-concepts/
Five core principles:
1. **Energy & Card Management**: tight on energy, costly powers. Prioritize card economy; skipping plays early to delay weak reclaim is valid.
2. **Growth Prioritization**: "Just ignore the top track besides the first fire element." Bottom track is the real engine.
3. **Presence Placement**: placing in already-blighted lands or creating sacred sites before ravages is sound even if slower early.
4. **Element Priority**: Plants > Fire. "Getting to 3 plant elements doubles the effectiveness of all fire you have."
5. **Blight Control Emphasis**: blight-management + defensive cards outweigh optimal element distributions.

**Opening 1 (Full bottom)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/wildfire-opening1/
- "Placing presence is effectively one of Wildfire's actions." Sequence carefully.
- Aim for early 2 Fire element; manage blight.
- Alternate "delayed 2 Fire" trades early damage for 3 CP earlier.

**Opening 2 (Early Major)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/wildfire-opening2/
- T1: G2 bottom + gain Major + forget Flash-Fires + Threatening Flames.
- T2: G3 top + Major or Asphyxiating Smoke.
- T3: G2 bottom + Flame's Fury + others.
- Useful vs. damage-resistant adversaries (England).

#### 5. Lure of the Deep Wilderness

**Opening 1 (G3 Elements & Energy)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/lure-opening1/
- Use G3 for Elements + Energy; bottom-track presence for CP.
- T1 Moon/Air, T2 Moon, T3 Reclaim.
- Only 3 additional cards gained all game — card variety is weak but play quality is strong.

**Opening 2 (Hybrid Major)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/lure-opening2/
- Early Major via G4.
- T1: G2 bottom. T2: G2 top + G4 Major. T3: reclaim.
- Plays Gift of the Untamed Wild + Softly Beckon Ever Inward.
- Target low-cost Moon/Plant Majors playable at 3 CP.

#### 6. Many Minds Move as One

**Opening 1 (Minors + Efficient Growth)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/mm-opening1/
- T1–2: G2 bottom × 2; T3: reclaim + Minor gain.
- T1 plays: Ever-Multiplying Swarm + another power. T2–3 scale to 3 cards/turn.
- 8→6 presence arc.
- "0-cost Powers should be prioritised."

**Opening 2 (Hybrid Majors)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/mm-opening2/
- R1 Ever-Multiplying Swarm + Guide the Way.
- R2–3: 3 cards/turn with element focus.
- G2 top+bottom → G2 bottom → G3.
- Secure 1 Air + 2 Animal elements early.
- Excess energy → extra Power gain.
- Weakness: 4-cost Majors only unlock T8.

#### 7. River Surges in Sunlight

**Opening 1**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/river-opening1/
- Bottom track; River's Bounty as cornerstone.
- Reach 2 Sun + 3 Water by T3.
- Reclaim River's Bounty every turn to fuel level 3 Massive Flooding by T4.
- "You need to play your full starting hand for max-level Massive Flooding."
- Transition to Majors T7–9 by forgetting Flash Floods.

#### 8. Sharp Fangs Behind the Leaves

**Concepts**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-concepts/
Five core principles:
1. **Growth Flexibility**: swap "G3 > G3" vs "G4 > G1" based on whether cards or reclaim is more valuable.
2. **Presence Conversion**: "Presence should be converted as much as possible into Beasts, ideally every turn." Keep only 2–4 presence on board.
3. **Beast Placement** (most critical): "the single most important aspect of playing Fangs." Positioning drives presence placement, innates, and future flexibility.
4. **Energy Management**: tight early. Best energy-generation is late-game unless sacrificing presence.
5. **Selective Minor Acquisition**: secure one specific elemental Minor first, then blight-handling.

**Opening 1 (Top track hybrid)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-opening1/
- G2 top; G3 Minor T1–2; T3 reclaim + Minor.
- 10 presence T1 → 6 by T5.
- Ranging Hunt active T2+ with 1 plant-animal + 2 animal elements.

**Opening 2 (Full bottom)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-opening2/
- G2 bottom; G3 Minor T1; G2 bottom; G4 T2; T3 reclaim + Minor.
- 3 CP on T2, early reclaim-one.
- No Majors — power diversity instead.

**Opening 3 (Hybrid with Majors)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-opening3/
- G2 top + G3 Minor T1–3.
- Acquire cost 2–5 Major by T3 (forget one).
- Plant+Animal and Animal triggers Ranging Hunt.
- Shift bottom track by T5 for 3 CP.

**Opening 4 (Reclaim-heavy Beast scaling)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-opening4/
- Growth on 2 and 4; constant reclaim.
- Teeth Gleam From Darkness + Too Near the Jungle every turn.
- "Very energy-starved" — requires 0-cost Minors.
- Strong vs. Base BP and Scotland.

#### 9. Shroud of Silent Mist

**Opening 1 (Stable Early Damage)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/mist-opening1/
- T1: The Fog Closes In + Forms Dart By; build Moon/Air/Water.
- T2: reclaim + Minor; activate left innate.
- Bottom-track presence growth T3–6. Slow scaling before T5.
- T3 goal: 3 damaged-invader lands + 1 Minor in hand.

**Opening 2 (Accelerated Mid-Game)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/mist-opening2/
- T1: Fog Closes In + Forms Dart By. T2: Unnerving Pall. T3: Dissolving Vapors.
- Bottom track primary; both tracks T1.
- Weak T2–3, accelerated card play by T4.
- Requires 0-cost Minors.

#### 10. Thunderspeaker

**Opening 1 (Hybrid Minors)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/thunderspeaker-opening1/
- T1 G3 bottom; T2 G2 top.
- T1 combos: Sudden Ambush + Manifestation (preserve targets) OR Manifestation + Voice of Thunder (unlock level 2).
- T2: Words of Warning + remaining.
- T3: reclaim + 2 Minors.
- Unlocks 4 Air by T5; 4 Sun by T7.

**Opening 2 (Energy-focused)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/thunderspeaker-opening2/
- G3 bottom + 2 Minors T1; Words of Warning T2; T3 reclaim + 2 more Minors.
- "Dependent on G3 for Energy, thus being limited in presence placement."
- Highest CP, late Air/Sun spikes.

**Opening 3 (Late-Game Scaling)**: https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/thunderspeaker-opening3/
- T1 G2 top + Sudden Ambush or Voice of Thunder.
- T2 G2 top + Words of Warning.
- T3 G3 bottom + remaining + Manifestation.
- Weak T1–2; 4 Air + 4 Sun + 3 CP by T5.
- Strong vs. slower adversaries like England.

## Part B: Rei's BoardGameGeek Guides

### Rei's canonical structure — applies to all spirit/aspect chapters

Rei's BGG guides share a section spine (with minor variation). This project adopts that structure:

1. **Spirit Overview / Framing** — identity, core fantasy, complexity signal, what the spirit "feels like"
2. **Core Mechanics & Special Rules** — each special rule and key innate explained *strategically*, not just as rules text
3. **Key Strategic Principles** ("Rei Says") — numbered list of 5–10 core principles that drive decisions
4. **Opening Strategy** — or cross-link to latentoctopus opening variants
5. **Element & Aspect Preferences** — which elements and aspects this spirit wants
6. **Card Priority Ratings** — letter grades (A+, A, A-, B+, …) with rationale per card
7. **Adversary Notes** — strong against / weak against with reasons
8. **Board Position Evaluation** — which maps/boards favor the spirit
9. **Game-Phase Strategy** (Early / Mid / Late)
10. **Multiplayer / Territory Management** — cooperation notes with blight-sensitive partners, etc.

### 1. Many Minds Move as One — Rei's Guide

- Primary: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2774198/guide-many-minds-move-as-one
- Advanced: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3030644/advanced-guide-many-minds-move-as-one

**Framing**: Spatial control through Beast management. Lots of Fear, Control, positioning. Strong synergy with Beast Majors. No offense to start — excellent stalling defense + Fear generation. Heavy spatial thought required for Beast movement (improved Push/Gather, large numbers of Beasts).

**Core Mechanics**:
- **Fly Fast as Thought**: Beasts gather/push up to 2 lands, not just adjacent. Dramatically changes tactics.
- **Sacred Sites as Beasts**: Sacred sites function as Beasts — effectively doubles presence effects when manipulated.
- **Unpredictability**: Fear Cards + Beasts events → higher variance than most spirits.

**Rei Says** (key principles):
1. Beast deployment is the core action — Beset and Confound scales 2-fear/defend-2 → 6-fear/defend-10 based on element combos.
2. Convert limited starting presence into a large Beast population early.
3. Air + Animal are primary elements.
4. Beast *positioning* is as important as quantity — use 2-range gather to concentrate Beasts on targeted lands.
5. Many Minds is a **fear farm** — scalable fear via Beasts, not direct damage.

**Adversary Notes**: Excels where Beast mobility + fear output outpace presence-placement requirements. Struggles where presence loss is punished or fear scoring is denied.

**Aspects**: Supports Animal/Air themes and Beast-scaling. Plant elements + early-growth aspects accelerate Beast deployment.

### 2. Shroud of Silent Mist — Rei's Guide

- Primary: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2722115/guide-shroud-of-silent-mist
- Advanced: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2722115/advanced-guide-shroud-silent-mist

**Framing**: "A very complicated Spirit." Persistent pressure through gradual damage accumulation rather than immediate threats. Constantly shifting/moving presence.

**Core Mechanics**:
- **Mists Shift and Flow**: gather presence into target or adjacent land to meet Range/targeting. Major tactical flexibility.
- **Slow and Silent Death (Innate)**: free Fear. "Its real strength."
- **Damaged invaders stay damaged** — cumulative damage + fear across turns, unlike other spirits.

**Rei Says**:
1. Gradual-pressure philosophy — not a fast-killer. Patience + positioning.
2. Presence mobility via Mists Shift and Flow is central.
3. Farm damaged invaders across multiple lands; maintain across turns.
4. Energy constrained — can "stretch to Major Powers" via Gather Power from the Cool and Dark if enough damaged-invader energy.
5. Fear is the primary win metric.
6. **Presence loss hurts Shroud more than most** — surround-and-envelop playstyle is presence-hungry. Aspects/minors offering presence refresh are valued.

**Adversary Notes**:
- Struggles vs. presence-removers (Russia), blight-preventers, fast adversaries.
- Excels vs. slower adversaries (England) where multi-turn setups pay off.

### 3. Vengeance as a Burning Plague — Rei's Guide

- Primary: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2709070/guide-vengeance-as-a-burning-plague
- Advanced: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2709070/advanced-guide-vengeance-burning-plague
- Podcast: https://spiriteddiscussion.substack.com/p/vengeance-as-a-burning-plague-with

**Author note**: Rei plays ~4 games/day, ~2 of 4 as Vengeance → 300+ career Vengeance games. Most experienced Vengeance player in the community.

**Framing**: "Not so powerful early, but can be a late-game juggernaut, especially if things are going badly: Blight adds to its Damage and its Presence being Destroyed adds Disease." Vengeance thrives where other spirits suffer — high-blight boards improve its position.

**Core Mechanics**:
- **Badlands mechanic**: all blight on the island functions as badlands → massive damage multipliers. "In a land with 2 Blight, this Spirit can destroy a City by dealing 1 Damage."
- **Disease stacking & scaling**: concentrate disease per land — amplifies Epidemics Run Rampant, card synergies, reclaim-phase damage.
- **Power gain on blight creation**: unique resource generation mechanism.

**Rei Says**:
1. **Invader preservation philosophy**: "Unlike most spirits, the aim is not entirely to destroy all the invaders as quickly as possible — instead you want to keep some of them alive for a bit."
2. **Concentrate disease, don't spread it**. 1 land with 4 disease >> 4 lands with 1 disease each.
3. **Blight is favorable**. Vengeance scales with island blight count.
4. **Ideal opening**: "place 2 presence and use Fiery Vengeance + Fetid Breath to nail an explorer and get some disease out."
5. **Reclaim-phase optimization** — highest-damage phase, plan around blight/disease multipliers.
6. **Territory in multiplayer**: struggles sharing space with blight-sensitive spirits (Sharp Fangs); coordinate separate territories with shared borders.

**Card Priority Ratings** (partial):
- Fetid Breath — A (disease accumulation)
- Plaguebearers — A (invader movement + disease)
- Strike Low With Sudden Fevers — A (conditional ravage-skip on disease)
- Epidemics Run Rampant triggers — high priority

**Element Preferences**: Fire + Water + Animal combos enable beast-themed thresholds (Sea Monsters, Angry Bears) that synergize with disease states.

**Board Position**: Rei rates **C, F, B** highly for disease concentration + invader manipulation. Boards with isolated invader clusters favor disease farming. Central boards ease spread + reclaim-phase damage.

**Adversary Notes**:
- Strong vs. sustained-blight adversaries, slow factions (Germany, England).
- Weak vs. fast/aggressive adversaries that prevent blight; spirits that remove blight.

**Game-Phase Arc**:
- T1–4: relatively weak, "punishing but rewarding," requires patience.
- T5–10: scaling accelerates as disease + blight multipliers activate.
- T10+: "late-game juggernaut" — exponential damage potential.
