## 1. Shadows Flicker Like Flame

- **Expansion**: Base
- **Complexity**: Low
- **Special Rules**: **Shadows of the Dahan** — whenever you use a Power, you may pay 1 Energy to target a land with Dahan regardless of the Power's Range.
- **Innate Powers**:
  - **Darkness Swallows the Unwary** — Fast, 1 Range from Sacred Site
    - L1: 2 Moon, 1 Fire → Gather 1 Explorer
    - L2: 3 Moon, 2 Fire → Destroy up to 2 Explorers; 1 Fear per destroyed
    - L3: 4 Moon, 3 Fire, 2 Air → 3 Damage; 1 Fear per destroyed Invader
- **Unique Cards**:
  - **Concealing Shadows** — 0E, (speed: unclear), target Land with Dahan, 1 Moon — Hide Dahan, preventing Ravage damage
  - **Crops Wither and Fade** — 0E, (speed: unclear), target Any, 1 Fire — Destroy 1 City or 2 Explorers *(Wiki said "Colonists"; verify)*
  - **Favors Called Due** — 1E, (speed: unclear), target Land with Dahan, 2 Moon + 1 Fire — Gather up to 3 Dahan; generate Fear
  - **Mantle of Dread** — 1E, Slow, target Land, 1 Moon — Gather 1 Explorer or remove Town
- **Aspects**: *(Wiki didn't specify; community lists Madness, Reach, Amorphous, Foreboding — verify before citing)*

## 2. Thunderspeaker

- **Expansion**: Base
- **Complexity**: Moderate
- **Special Rules**:
  - **Ally of the Dahan** — your Presence may move with Dahan (move 1 Presence whenever Dahan moves from one of your lands to another).
  - **Sworn to Victory** — after a Ravage Action destroys 1 or more Dahan, for each Dahan destroyed, destroy 1 of your Presence within 1 Range.
- **Innate Powers**:
  - **Gather the Warriors** — Slow (can be Fast), 1 Range, Any land — 4 Air + 1 Animal → Gather up to 1 Dahan per Air; Push up to 1 Dahan per Sun
  - **Lead the Furious Assault** — Slow (can be Fast), 0 Range, Any land
    - 2 Sun, 1 Fire → Destroy 1 Town for every 2 Dahan in target land
    - 4 Sun, 3 Fire → Destroy 1 City for every 3 Dahan in target land
- **Unique Cards**: Manifestation of Power and Glory, Sudden Ambush, Voice of Thunder, Words of Warning *(Wiki didn't list full text details for all; verify each)*
- **Aspects**: *(Wiki didn't list; community names include Warrior, Tactician, Mentor — verify)*

## 3. Ocean's Hungry Grasp

- **Expansion**: Base
- **Complexity**: High
- **Special Rules**: Presence may add/move into Oceans, not Inland lands. Invaders/Dahan moved to oceans are drowned; spirit exchanges drowned invader health for energy (X health = 1 energy, where X = player count).
- **Innate Powers**:
  - **Pound Ships to Splinters** — Fast, 0 Range, Coastal
    - 1 Moon, 1 Air, 2 Water → 1 Fear
    - 2 Moon, 1 Air, 3 Water → +1 Fear (total 2)
    - 3 Moon, 2 Air, 4 Water → +2 Fear (total 3)
  - **Ocean Breaks the Shore** — Slow, 0 Range, Coastal
    - 2 Water, 1 Earth → Drown 1 Town
    - 3 Water, 2 Earth → May Drown 1 City instead
    - 4 Water, 3 Earth → Also Drown 1 Town/City
- **Unique Cards**: Call of the Deeps (0E, Fast, 0 Range Coastal, requires Sacred Site), Grasping Tide, Swallow the Land-Dwellers, Tidal Boon
- **Aspects**: *(not listed in source)*

## 4. A Spread of Rampant Green

- **Expansion**: Base
- **Complexity**: Moderate
- **Special Rules**:
  - **Choke the Land with Green** — whenever Invaders would Ravage or Build in a land with your Sacred Site, you may prevent it by destroying one of your Presence in that land.
  - **Steady Regeneration** — destroyed Presence can be restored during Growth (freely on Healthy islands; 1 Energy per Presence on Blighted islands).
- **Innate Powers**:
  - **Creepers Tear into Mortar** — Slow, 0 Range, Any land
    - 1 Moon, 2 Plant → 1 damage to 1 Town/City
    - 2 Moon, 3 Plant → Repeat this Power
    - 3 Moon, 4 Plant → Repeat this Power again
  - **All-Enveloping Green** — Fast, 1 Range from Sacred Site, Any land
    - 1 Water, 3 Plant → Defend 2
    - 2 Water, 4 Plant → Defend 4 instead
    - 3 Water, 1 Earth, 5 Plant → Defend 4 and remove 1 Blight
- **Unique Cards**: Fields Choked with Growth, Gift of Proliferation, Overgrow in a Night, Stem the Flow of Fresh Water
- **Aspects**: *(not listed in source)*

## 5. Bringer of Dreams and Nightmares

- **Expansion**: *(not specified; confirmed Base in other sources)*
- **Complexity**: High
- **Special Rules**: **To Dream a Thousand Deaths** — Powers never cause actual Damage or Destruction (except to own Presence). Damage that would destroy Explorers/Towns/Cities converts to 0/2/5 Fear respectively; Invaders are Pushed instead.
- **Innate Powers**:
  - **Spirits May Yet Dream** — Fast, target Any Spirit
    - 2 Moon, 2 Air → Reveal a face-down Fear Card
    - 3 Moon → Target Spirit gains an element they already possess
  - **Night Terrors** — Fast, 0 Range
    - 1 Moon, 1 Air → 1 Fear
    - 2 Moon, 1 Air, 1 Animal → +1 Fear
    - 3 Moon, 2 Air, 1 Animal → +1 Fear
- **Unique Cards**:
  - **Call on Midnight's Dream** — 0E, Fast, Moon + Air — Generate 2 Fear OR gain a Major Power (forget this card as payment)
  - **Dread Apparitions** — 1E, Fast, 2 Moon + 1 Air + 1 Animal — +1 Fear and Defend 1 in target land with Invaders
  - **Dreams of the Dahan** — 1E, Fast, 2 Moon + 1 Air — Move Dahan to land OR generate Fear based on Dahan count
  - **Predatory Nightmares** — 2E, Fast, Moon + Fire — Move up to 2 Dahan away; convert 2 Damage into Fear/Pushes
- **Aspects**: *(not listed)*

## 10. Keeper of the Forbidden Wilds

- **Expansion**: *(not specified; confirmed Branch & Claw in other sources)*
- **Complexity**: Moderate
- **Special Rules**: **Forbidden Ground** — after you create a Sacred Site, Push all Dahan from that land. Dahan Events never move Dahan to your Sacred Site, but Powers can do so.
- **Innate Powers**:
  - **Punish Those Who Trespass** — Slow, 0 Range, Any land
    - Base (2 Sun, 1 Fire, 2 Plant): 2 Damage; Destroy 1 Dahan
    - Upgrade (2 Sun, 2 Fire, 3 Plant): +1 Damage per Sun/Plant you have
    - Upgrade (4 Plant): Split this Power's Damage between target land and another 1 of your lands
  - **Spreading Wilds** — Slow, 1 Range, Land without Blight
    - (2 Sun): Push 1 Explorer per 2 Sun you have
    - (1 Plant): If target has no Explorer, add 1 Wilds
    - (3 Plant): +1 Range
    - (1 Air): +1 Range
- **Unique Cards**: Boon of Growing Power, Regrow from Roots, Sacrosanct Wilderness, Towering Wrath *(NOT "Gift of the Forbidden Wilds", "Voracious Growth", "Forbidden Paths", "Boon of Vigor" — those were hallucinated)*
- **Aspects**: *(not listed)*

## 11. Serpent Slumbering Beneath the Island

- **Expansion**: *(not specified; confirmed Feather & Flame promo in other sources)*
- **Complexity**: High
- **Special Rules**: **Deep Slumber** — starts limited to 5 Presence on island. "Absorb Essence" raises this limit (5→7→8→10→11→12→13).
- **Innate Powers**:
  - **The Serpent Wakes in Power** — Slow, 0 Range, Self
    - L1: 2 Fire, 1 Water, 1 Plant → Gain 1 Energy; other spirits with absorbed Presence gain 1 Energy
    - L2: 2 Water, 3 Earth, 2 Plant → Add 1 Presence to 1 Range; others with 2+ absorbed Presence may do likewise
    - L3: 3 Fire, 3 Water, 3 Earth, 3 Plant → Gain a Major Power without Forgetting; others with 3+ absorbed Presence may do likewise
  - **The Serpent Rouses in Anger** — Slow, 0 Range, Any target land
    - L1: 1 Fire, 1 Earth → 1 Damage per Fire/Earth pair to Towns/Cities
    - L2: 2 Moon, 2 Earth → 2 Fear per Moon/Earth pair; may Push 1 Town from target land
    - L3: 5 Moon, 6 Fire, 6 Earth → X Damage across all lands (X = Presence in/adjacent to each land)
- **Unique Cards**:
  - **Absorb Essence** — 2E, Fast, Self-target, 2 Earth + 3 Water + 3 Fire — Removes another spirit's Presence; they gain Energy and elements; benefits "Wakes in Power" innate
  - **Elemental Aegis** — Slow, Self-target, 3 Earth + 3 Water + 3 Fire — Defends multiple lands; strength scales with absorbed Presence
  - **Gift of Flowing Power** — Affects other spirits; provides Power Card play opportunity
  - **Gift of the Primordial Deeps** — Affects other spirits; provides elemental/play benefits

*(NOT "Burned Lands Boiling Seas", "Pent-Up Calamity", "Shadows of the Dreamer", "Awaken the Watcher" — those were all hallucinated.)*

## 12. Finder of Paths Unseen

- **Expansion**: *(not specified; Promo Pack 2)*
- **Complexity**: Very High
- **Special Rules**:
  - **Responsibilities to the Dead** — following destruction actions, lose 1 Presence and 1 Energy (or destroy another Presence if lacking energy).
  - **Open the Ways** — you may make up to two of your lands adjacent at a time. You may change which lands are adjacent once between Actions.
- **Innate Powers**:
  - **Lay Paths They Cannot Help But Walk** — Fast, 0 Range, Any Land
    - 2 Moon, 2 Air → Push up to half (rounded down) of Invaders from target land; do likewise for Dahan, Presence, and Beasts
    - 2 Sun, 2 Air → Push up to 1 Invader/Dahan/Presence/Beasts
    - 2 Moon, 4 Air, 3 Water → Repeat this Power
  - **Close the Ways** — Fast, 1 Range, Any Land (each level targets separately)
    - 1 Air, 2 Water → Isolate target land
    - 2 Air, 2 Earth → Isolate target land
    - 3 Air, 2 Plant → Isolate target land
- **Unique Cards** (Finder has more uniques than most spirits): A Circuitous and Wending Journey, Aid from the Spirit-Speakers, Offer Passage Between Worlds, Paths Tied by Nature, Traveler's Boon, Ways of Shore and Heartland

*(NOT "Bearer of Unwanted Burdens", "Quicken the Earth's Struggles", "Pact of Untamed Wilderness", "Uncanny Melting of Form" — those were all hallucinated.)*
