## Spirit Overview — Framing

Fangs is the **beast-scaling damage spirit** — the island's apex predators. You convert presence into beasts each turn; beasts damage invaders via Ranging Hunt; beasts also enable innate firings and serve as positional anchors for powers. Fangs plays tight-energy, high-CP, constantly-threatening.

**One-line fantasy**: the jungle hunts. Invaders never know when the pack strikes; every shadow is teeth.

**The honest complexity signal**: Moderate is correct. Fangs has the most opening variants of any base-or-B&C spirit (4 documented by latentoctopus). The spirit's mechanics are simple; its *position-management* complexity is real — beast placement is described as "the single most important aspect" in community guides.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules (from latentoctopus concepts)

Per latentoctopus's 5 core principles:

1. **Growth flexibility**: swap "G3 > G3" vs "G4 > G1" based on cards vs reclaim value.
2. **Presence conversion priority**: "Presence should be converted as much as possible into Beasts, ideally every turn." Keep only 2–4 presence on board.
3. **Beast placement strategy** (most critical): "the single most important aspect." Beast positioning drives everything.
4. **Energy management**: tight early. Best energy generation is late-game unless sacrificing presence.
5. **Selective Minor acquisition**: one specific elemental Minor first (Plant-Animal dual for Ranging Hunt), then blight-handling.

### Innate: Ranging Hunt

Core damage. Activated by 1 plant-animal element + 2 Animal elements from T2 onward.

### Key Uniques

- **Teeth Gleam From Darkness** — Beast creation.
- **Too Near the Jungle** — Push + Beast.
- **Elusive Ways of the Predator** — Speed + Beast.
- **Stir the Trees and Stones** — Damage + Beast.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Convert presence → beasts every turn.** Presence on the spirit panel is wasted; beasts on the board are damage.
2. **Plant-Animal dual element is the trigger.** Draft for it.
3. **Beast positioning matters more than quantity.** 3 beasts in one land = 6 damage; 3 beasts in 3 lands = 2 damage each.
4. **Energy-tight.** Expect 1–2E through T5; draft 0-cost Minors.
5. **Skip Majors unless Opening 3 (hybrid-Major).** Fangs is Minor-heavy by default.
6. **Jungle + Mountain preferred.** Beast deployment scales with terrain.
7. **Avoid sacred-site density.** Beasts spread; don't concentrate presence.

```admonish tip title="Pro Tip"
Before Fast powers, count beasts in Ranging Hunt range. If 2+ beasts in a ravage land, you can skip the kill-card — beasts handle it during ravage retaliation.
```

## Opening Strategy (4 latentoctopus variants)

### Opening A — Top Track Hybrid 🟨

**Source**: [latentoctopus — Fangs Opening 1](https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-opening1/)

**Target arc**: G2 top; G3 Minor T1-T2; T3 reclaim + Minor. Ranging Hunt active T2+ with 1 plant-animal + 2 Animal elements.

- **T1**: G2 top + G3 Minor; play Teeth Gleam + 0-cost Minor. 10 presence → 9.
- **T2**: G2 top + G3 Minor; Teeth Gleam reclaimed + 2nd Minor.
- **T3**: G1 Reclaim + G3 Minor; 3 cards total.
- **T4 audit**: 6 presence on spirit; 3–4 beasts placed; Ranging Hunt firing.

### Opening B — Full Bottom 🟨

**Source**: [latentoctopus — Fangs Opening 2](https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-opening2/)

**When**: build-focused adversaries where CP + reclaim-one scaling matters.

Bottom-track focus: G2 bottom + G3 Minor T1; G2 bottom + G4 T2; reclaim + Minor T3. Achieves 3CP on T2. Energy-tighter than Opening A. 1-cost Minor selection critical.

### Opening C — Hybrid with Majors 🟨

**Source**: [latentoctopus — Fangs Opening 3](https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-opening3/)

**When**: against adversaries with health-increases or excessive blight.

G2 top + G3 Minor each turn. Acquire 2-5 cost Major by T3 (forget Minor unique). Maintain Ranging Hunt through element management.

### Opening D — Reclaim-Heavy Beast Scaling 🟥

**Source**: [latentoctopus — Fangs Opening 4](https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/fangs-opening4/)

**When**: Base Brandenburg-Prussia, Scotland — specific invader factions.

Growth on 2 and 4; constant reclaim. "Very energy-starved." Strong Beast scaling; limited flexibility.

**Preferred elements**: Plant-Animal dual (essential), Animal (secondary), Plant (tertiary).

## Board Position Evaluation

- **Favorable**: Jungle + Mountain dense boards.
- **Neutral**: Most.
- **Unfavorable**: Coastal-heavy or sparse-jungle.

## Game-Phase Strategy

- **Early (T1–3)**: convert presence to beasts; draft Plant-Animal dual.
- **Mid (T4–6)**: Ranging Hunt every turn; 2-3 CP.
- **Late (T7+)**: close via beast-retaliation + innate + (Opening C only) Major.

## Synergy Partners

```admonish tip title="Best Partners"
- **Thunderspeaker** — dahan + beasts share battlefield.
- **River** — River pushes; beasts bite the displaced invaders.
- **Earth** — Earth defends; Fangs damages.
- **Bringer** — Bringer fear-scales; Fangs kills for fear spike.
```

```admonish warning title="Anti-Synergy"
- **Wildfire** — blight compatibility but competes for territory.
- **Vengeance** — blight-alive-invaders vs. Fangs kill-fast.
```

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Not converting presence to beasts. Presence on the panel is inert; beasts on board do damage.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Spreading beasts thinly. 3 beasts in 1 land > 3 beasts in 3 lands.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Drafting 1-cost Minors on Opening B (Full bottom). Energy is already tight.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Playing Opening C without a matching Major in offer. Forgets are wasted.
```
