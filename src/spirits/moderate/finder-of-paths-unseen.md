## Spirit Overview — Framing

Finder is the **mobility spirit**. Vision tokens + presence-relocation mechanics let Finder reach any land with any power, regardless of normal range rules. Finder is simultaneously the island's chart-maker (vision reveals future invader moves) and its mover (push/gather/teleport invaders between lands).

**One-line fantasy**: the hidden paths. Finder sees where the enemy will be; Finder rewrites where they can go.

**The honest complexity signal**: Play Difficulty 3 is correct. Finder is **strategically deep** — every turn has exotic options (re-route an Explore, cross-board target a Major, relocate your own presence mid-play). New players miss options because the mechanics expand beyond standard range rules.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Special Rule: Vision Tokens

Finder places vision tokens that reveal invader intents. Vision-adjacent targeting extends Finder's effective range.

### Innate: Many Paths Lead Onward

Mobility-scaling damage + range extension. Moon-Air threshold heavy.

### Key Uniques

- **Bearer of Unwanted Burdens** — presence relocation.
- **Quicken the Earth's Struggles** — mobility + damage.
- **Pact of Untamed Wilderness** — dahan-beast-terrain movement.
- **Uncanny Melting of Form** — transformation / extreme mobility.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Vision tokens first.** Place vision early for future-turn cross-targeting.
2. **Presence relocation is a weapon.** Bearer lets Finder target distant lands by moving presence adjacent mid-play.
3. **Moon + Air threshold.** Primary elements; Water tertiary.
4. **Don't clump presence.** Finder's mobility wants presence spread; sacred-site density is wasted.
5. **Re-route invader explores.** Finder can cancel an Explore by moving the source; high-leverage play.
6. **Partner with slow-damagers.** Finder reaches; Major-shopping partners damage.
7. **Aspect flexibility** — community meta evolving for Finder aspects.

## Opening Strategy

### Opening A — Vision-focused 🟨

**Target arc**: Vision deployed T1–T2 · Level 2 innate T4 · Mobility-kill chain T5+.

#### Turn 1

- **Growth**: **G2 bottom** (presence + card play).
- **Cards played**: **Bearer of Unwanted Burdens + a Minor**.
- **Presence placement**: central land for vision coverage.
- **Elements by end**: 1 Moon, 1 Air.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: vision deployed; first relocation capability.

#### Turn 2

- **Growth**: **G3** (gain Minor).
- **Cards played**: **Quicken the Earth's Struggles + Minor**.
- **Presence placement**: adjacent to vision.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Moon, 1 Air.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: 2 vision tokens; mobility established.

#### Turn 3

- **Growth**: **G1 Reclaim + G3 Minor**.
- **Cards played**: **3 cards** (2 Uniques + Minor).
- **Presence placement**: third land.
- **Elements by end**: 3 Moon, 2 Air.
- **E / CP state**: 2E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: Level 2 innate threshold; 1 kill via mobility.

#### Turn 4 — state audit

After T3:
- **Presence**: 6 on board; spread.
- **Energy / CP**: 2E / 3CP.
- **Engine**: vision + mobility working.
- **Fear pool**: 3 of 8.
- **Blight**: 0–1.

### Opening B — Early Major 🟥

**When**: experimental for experienced Finder players.

- T1–T2 standard.
- T3: G4 Major gain (forget a weak Unique).
- T4: Major fires.

## Board Position Evaluation

- **Favorable**: Central-island boards; vision coverage broad.
- **Neutral**: Most boards.
- **Unfavorable**: Fragmented layouts; mobility less valuable.

## Game-Phase Strategy

- **Early (T1–3)**: Vision + mobility setup.
- **Mid (T4–6)**: Level 2 innate; mobility-kill chains.
- **Late (T7+)**: Terror 2 flip; mobility closes distant lands.

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Not using vision tokens for cross-range targeting. Vision is Finder's main mechanical advantage.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Over-concentrating presence. Finder wants spread for maximum range coverage.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Ignoring the re-route Explore option. Canceling an Explore via source movement is a high-leverage Finder-specific play.
```
