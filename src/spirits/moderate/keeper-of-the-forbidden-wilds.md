## Spirit Overview — Framing

Keeper is the **premier Major Power Shopper**. Sacred-site-density + high energy income support 4-cost+ Majors landing T3 onward. Where Earth's Major engine fires T5+, Keeper's fires T3–T4. Keeper is also a capable defender via blight removal + sacred-site effects.

**One-line fantasy**: the forest is watching. The forest remembers. What the forest forbids, the invaders find impassable.

**The honest complexity signal**: Moderate officially. Keeper's complexity is in **sacred-site placement** — the spirit's innates and power cards reward density, but density costs presence flexibility. Mid-level players over-concentrate; top-level players spread just enough to threshold multiple lands.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Innate: Ancient Splinter of Wisdom

Sacred-site-scaling damage + blight removal. Sun + Plant + Earth thresholds.

### Boon of Vigor (Unique)

Energy + dahan + innate trigger.

### Voracious Growth (Unique)

Terrain-shaping + blight removal. Keeper's signature blight-handling card.

### Gift of the Forbidden Wilds (Unique)

Fast defend + dahan support.

### Forbidden Paths (Unique)

Movement + terrain disruption.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Major Power early.** Aim for first Major T2–T3 via G4 on a growth-rich turn.
2. **Sacred-site density matters.** 2 presence per land in 2–3 lands = Level 2 innate.
3. **Blight removal is Keeper's edge.** Voracious Growth + Plant-element innate scaling heals blight consistently.
4. **Sun-Plant-Earth threshold spread.** Level 3 innate requires all three; draft accordingly.
5. **Keeper defends.** Not just offensive Majors — Gift of the Forbidden Wilds + sacred-site effects cover dahan + lands.
6. **Don't forget load-bearing Uniques.** Unlike Earth's Draw, Keeper's Uniques are more valuable; careful Forget choice.
7. **Jungle + mountain boards preferred.** Sacred-site terrain.

## Opening Strategy

### Opening A — Slow-ramp into Early Major 🟨

**Target arc**: Major T2–T3 · Level 2 innate T4 · 3 Majors played by T8.

#### Turn 1

- **Growth**: **G2 top** (energy + card play).
- **Cards played**: **Gift of the Forbidden Wilds + Boon of Vigor**.
- **Presence placement**: first sacred-site land (jungle or mountain).
- **Elements by end**: 1 Sun, 1 Plant.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: defend active; 1E banked via Boon.

#### Turn 2

- **Growth**: **G4 Major gain**. Forget Forbidden Paths (or equivalent weaker unique). Gain Plant-Sun Major (Vigor of the Breaking Dawn, Gift of the Untamed Wild, Tigers Hunting).
- **Cards played**: **Gift reclaimed + a Minor**.
- **Presence placement**: second land; sacred site forming.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Sun, 1 Plant, 1 Earth.
- **E / CP state**: 2E / 2CP → 1E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: Major in hand; sacred site prep.

#### Turn 3

- **Growth**: **G1 Reclaim** (refresh hand including Major).
- **Cards played**: **Major + Minor**. First Major fires.
- **Presence placement**: third land; sacred site complete.
- **Elements by end**: 3 Sun, 2 Plant, 1 Earth.
- **E / CP state**: 3E / 2CP → 1–2E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: Major fires; fear spike; Level 2 innate threshold.

#### Turn 4 — state audit

After T3:
- **Presence**: 5–6 on board; 1–2 sacred sites.
- **Energy / CP**: 2–3E / 3CP.
- **Engine**: Major in rotation; Level 2 innate firing.
- **Fear pool**: 3 of 8.
- **Blight**: 0–1.

Pivot advice:
- **Wrong Major offered T2** → delay Major; take G2 instead; gain Major T4.
- **Sacred site blocked by ravage** → shift placement to adjacent land.
- **Energy spike needed** → G1 top for safety bank.

### Opening B — Defend-heavy, slow Major 🟨

**When**: Russia L5+, Habsburg Mining L5+; defensive posture required.

- T1–T2: Gift + reclaim; bank energy.
- T3: G4 Major gain.
- T4: Major fires.

## Element & Aspect Preferences

**Preferred elements**: Sun (primary), Plant (secondary), Earth (tertiary).

**Aspects**: Hostility (more aggressive damage) · Spreading Hostility (viral adversary disruption). Both shift toward damage over defense.

## Board Position Evaluation

- **Favorable**: Jungle + Mountain sacred-site density.
- **Neutral**: Most boards.
- **Unfavorable**: Coastal-heavy layouts.

## Game-Phase Strategy

- **Early (T1–3)**: Gift + reclaim; gain Major T2–T3; sacred-site formation.
- **Mid (T4–6)**: Major in rotation; Level 2 innate; second Major gain.
- **Late (T7+)**: 2–3 Majors; close via kill-chain.

## Synergy Partners

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Forgetting a load-bearing Unique. Keeper's Uniques are generally strong; picky Forget choices.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Drafting Majors without Sun-Plant threshold. Most Keeper Majors want both; missing one halves the card.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Over-concentrating presence in 1 land. Sacred sites scale Level 2 better across 2–3 lands.
```
