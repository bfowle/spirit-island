## Spirit Overview — Framing

Rising Heat is a **weaken-and-destroy board controller** — Blistering Heat gives Invaders −1 HP at your Sacred Sites (a party-wide damage multiplier helping every spirit's damage cards at Heat's Sacred Sites). The innate is one of the strongest HoSI innates — capable of wiping an entire land at tier 4.

**Wiki-printed playstyle note**:

> Sacred-site damage multiplier + range-1 innate scaling. Teaches Sacred-Site management.

**Teaching role**: Sacred-Site management, range-1 planning, innate-centric play.

## Starting Setup

> Put **2 Presence** on your starting board, in the **highest-numbered Sands**. You start with your **4 Unique Power Cards and 0 Energy**.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Special Rule: Blistering Heat

> At your Sacred Site, Invaders have −1 Health (min. 1).

Party-wide damage multiplier. Every damage Power at a Heat Sacred Site hits harder. In multi-spirit, partners' damage Powers benefit too.

## Unique Cards (all 4, Wiki-verified names)

- **Sweltering Exhaustion** — S-tier skip card; prevents Build.
- **Call on Herders for Aid** — flexibility.
- **Stinging Sandstorm** — push/damage utility.
- **Gift of Searing Heat** (shared HoSI unique) — 1 Damage at Sacred Sites across the table.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Fire + Earth drafts.** Earth comes from tracks; Fire comes from Minors.
2. **Sweltering Exhaustion is S-tier** — Range-2 from Sacred Site; skips Builds.
3. **0-cost cards are king.** Starting 0E is tight.
4. **Sacred Site placement drives Sweltering Exhaustion reach.** Plan inland coverage.
5. **In multi-spirit, give Gift of Searing Heat to the right ally** — amplified by Blistering Heat.

## Possible Openings

### Shared starting state

- **2 Presence** in highest Sands.
- **4 Uniques** in hand.
- **0 Energy**, 1 Card Play.

### Opening A — Standard G3 🟨 (default)

**T1 · Growth**: G3 + Minor (Fire priority — ~93% hit rate by 4 drafts).
**T1 · Play**: Call on Herders + any; hits innate L1 (2F/2A).

**T2 · Growth**: G3 + Minor.
**T2 · Play**: Aim for tier-2 (F3/E2 → 2 Damage).

**T3 · Growth**: G3 + Minor, or swap to G2 bot/bot for 4 plays.

**T4 · Growth**: G1 Reclaim + Minor.

### Opening B — Fast Tempo 🟥

Skip G3 for early Minor spam; faster board presence.

### Opening C — Early Major 🟥

Grab a Major by T3 on big Energy gain — Earth-rich presence makes this cheap.

### Opening Decision

- **Default Opening A** — most consistent.
- **Opening B / C** for experienced players.

## Board / Map Configuration

Best: **G** (5 adjacent starting positions from highest-Sands — ideal G3 range). **H** mid — workable.

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Named mistakes"
1. **Overspending Energy.** 0-cost cards are king.
2. **Accepting 1 Damage from Gift of Searing Heat** — always have a Defend or presence-safe target.
3. **Sacred Site placement ignoring Sweltering Exhaustion's range-2 reach.** Plan inland coverage.
4. **Pushing AND killing the same Explorer** — wasted tempo.
```

## Synergy Partners (Multiplayer)

- **Finder of Paths Unseen** — shared interest in tall problem lands + skip effects.
- **Bringer of Dreams and Nightmares** — Blistering Heat's lower-HP mobs die easier to fear-kill Fear cards.
- **Lightning** — makes slow innate Fast.
- **Weaker with pure defenders** — Heat wants Invaders alive long enough to Sacred-Site them.
