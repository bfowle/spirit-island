## Spirit Overview — Framing

Mud is a **slow oozing blight-control / anti-Build spirit** — Offer No Firm Foundations makes Builds at your Sacred Sites add Explorers instead of Towns/Cities (a soft Control lock). The innate *moves* presence onto new lands — every turn Mud is physically repositioning its Sacred Sites while inflicting Fear + Damage + Push.

**Wiki-printed playstyle note**:

> Presence-in-motion mechanic. Breaks new-player expectations about Sacred Sites being static.

**Teaching role**: unusual presence-mobility; introduces "Sacred Sites move with the spirit."

## Starting Setup

> Put **2 Presence** on your starting board, in the **lowest-numbered Wetland**. You start with your **4 Unique Powers and 0 Energy**.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Special Rule: Offer No Firm Foundations

> At your Sacred Site, Build actions add Explorer instead of Town/City.

**Soft Build-prevention** — Builds at your sacred sites add an Explorer instead of a Town/City. Every Build turn where a Sacred Site catches a Build = free Fear-upgrade loss prevention.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Moon + Water is the spine.** Earth caps the innate. Only 2 starting cards have Moon — priority draft.
2. **Every Ravage turn, keep one Sacred Site under a Build land** so Offer No Firm Foundations downgrades a Town/City to an Explorer.
3. **L1 move is required.** Plan presence so the move lands usefully.
4. **Majors are viable** via Exaltation (Aaron Stevens BGG 2959811).
5. **Drop a Dahan defensively via Open Shifting Waterways** — shift presence to a future innate target.

## Possible Openings

### Shared starting state

- **2 Presence** in lowest Wetland.
- **4 Uniques** in hand.
- **0 Energy**, 1 Card Play.

### Opening A — taurunti Mixed 🟨

**T1 · Growth**: G2 (top/top).
**T1 · Play**: **Open Shifting Waterways** — place Dahan defensively + shift presence. Use innate to reposition.

**T2 · Growth**: G3 bot (+P1, +P1, +2E).
**T2 · Play**: **Minor + Unique** (Exaltation or Foul Vapors). Grow *into* the shifted land to refresh Sacred-Site coverage.

**T3 · Growth**: G2 bot/bot, play all cards.

**T4 · Growth**: G1 Reclaim top (keep hand intact, reset energy curve) unless you need extra Moon for innate upgrade.

**Target**: Sustain innate L2 (M1/W2/E2) every turn after T4.

### Opening Decision

- **Default Opening A** — Aaron Stevens' "goal build" approach works here.

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Named mistakes"
1. **Forgetting L1 innate is mandatory** — you move 1 presence off Sacred Site collapsing it.
2. **Placing Sacred Sites far from Build lands** — Offer No Firm Foundations never triggers.
3. **Panicking at T1/T2 when only hitting tier 1–2** — don't draft Fire cards that don't fire the innate.
```
