## Spirit Overview — Framing

Green is the **terrain-shaping defender** that grows into a Major-Power closer. Early game, Green's wild growth covers lands in Plant thresholds while defending against ravages. Mid game, Majors start landing — Vigor of the Breaking Dawn, Entwined Power, Tigers Hunting — and Green turns into a damage engine wrapped in defense.

**One-line fantasy**: the jungle reclaims. What was a ravaged land becomes overgrown, invaders drowned in root-systems, dahan safe under the canopy.

**The honest complexity signal**: printed Low, but the Major-shopping dimension adds Moderate depth. Early-game Green is simple (defend + wild growth); late-game requires threshold planning across 3-Plant, 2-Water, 1-Earth combinations for optimal Major firings. The jump from "simple spirit" to "Major-shopper" is where the complexity lives.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Innate: Overgrow Destruction (Wild Growth)

Adds blight removal + Plant scaling. Level 1 is modest; Level 2 is transformative.

- **Level 1 (2 Plant)**: prevent 1 blight in target land.
- **Level 2 (3 Plant, 2 Water)**: prevent + remove existing blight; scales with dahan count.

### Innate: Twilight by Tangled Paths

Terrain-shaping; moves invaders via Plant effects. Supports terrain-control archetype.

### Gift of Proliferation (Unique)

Scaling presence + ally spread. Core Plant-generator.

### Sprout Lands into Battleground (Unique, aspect-dependent)

Terrain-shaping combat power. Aspect-gated; base Green has a different version.

### Fields Choked with Growth (Unique)

Defend + Plant threshold fuel.

### Entwined Power (Unique)

Power-gifting unique; supports partner Spirit in multiplayer.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Plant elements are everything.** Green's innates + Major threshold all want Plant; draft Plant-bearing Minors aggressively early.
2. **Blight removal is a real weapon.** Level 2 innate removes existing blight; combined with defend, Green can stabilize a pre-cascaded land.
3. **Majors come early.** Green is Major-heavy; forget Fields Choked with Growth (or equivalent weak Unique) T2–T4 for a Plant-threshold Major.
4. **Coordinate with partners in multiplayer.** Entwined Power gifts cards; designate a partner to receive Green's energy-extended plays.
5. **Aspect choice matters.** Base Green vs. Regrowth vs. Tangles produce substantially different games.
6. **Don't over-focus on damage.** Green is not a primary damage spirit; its value is terrain + defend + blight-heal. Majors add damage; Uniques primarily sustain.
7. **Wetland + jungle preference.** Presence placed in wetland/jungle lands scales Plant innates better.

```admonish tip title="Pro Tip"
Before gaining a Major, verify you can hit its Plant threshold consistently within 2 turns. Vigor of the Breaking Dawn (3 Plant, 2 Earth, 1 Sun) may require specific Minor drafts to threshold reliably.
```

## Opening Strategy

### Opening A — Full bottom, Early Major 🟨

**When to pick this**: default. Works across most adversaries.

**Target arc**: Major T3 · Level 2 innate T4 · Terror 2 flip by T6.

#### Turn 1

- **Growth**: **G3 bottom** (place presence + Minor gain + energy bank).
- **Cards played**: **Gift of Proliferation + Fields Choked with Growth**.
- **Presence placement**: wetland or jungle land.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Plant, 1 Water.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: Level 1 innate prep; 1 Plant Minor in hand.

#### Turn 2

- **Growth**: **G4 Major gain** (forget Fields Choked for a Plant Major — Entwined Power, Tigers Hunting, or Vigor of the Breaking Dawn if offered).
- **Cards played**: **Gift of Proliferation reclaimed + new Minor**.
- **Presence placement**: second land.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Plant, 2 Water.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: Major in hand; forget complete; 2 Minors drafted.

#### Turn 3

- **Growth**: **G1 Reclaim** (refresh hand including Major).
- **Cards played**: **Major + a Minor**. First Major fires.
- **Presence placement**: third land.
- **Elements by end**: 3 Plant, 2 Water (Level 2 threshold).
- **E / CP state**: 2E / 2CP → 0–1E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: Major fires; Level 2 innate available; fear spike from Major + innate.

#### Turn 4 — state audit

After T3:

- **Presence**: 6–7 of 13; jungle/wetland clustered.
- **Energy / CP**: 2–3E / 2–3CP.
- **Engine state**: 1 Major in rotation; Level 2 innate firing.
- **Fear pool**: 3–4 of 8.
- **Blight**: 0–1 (Level 2 removes as it fires).

Pivot advice:
- **Wrong Major offered T2** → skip G4 on T2; take G3 instead; delay Major to T4.
- **Missing Plant element** → 2 Plant via Unique only means Level 2 delayed to T5.
- **Multiplayer partner needs Entwined Power** → play it; don't hoard.

### Opening B — Minor-heavy terrain control 🟨

**When to pick this**: adversaries with heavy terrain-targeting (Habsburg Livestock, some scenarios).

- **T1**: G3 bottom + Gift of Proliferation + Minor.
- **T2**: G2 bottom + 2 Minors drafted.
- **T3**: Reclaim; focus on Plant-element Minors stacking innate firings.
- **T4 audit**: 3 Minors + Level 2 innate; no Major yet; scaling via innate rather than Major.

Opening B is slower but more resilient vs. card-pool variance.

## Element & Aspect Preferences

**Preferred elements**: Plant (primary), Water (secondary), Earth (tertiary for specific Majors).

**Aspects**:

- **Regrowth** — heavy blight-removal focus; strong vs. Wildfire-partner or late-blight adversaries.
- **Tangles** — terrain-control emphasis; more movement + push.

**Pick default**: base Green for first games; Regrowth against blight-heavy matchups.

## Card Priority Ratings

Green is **Major-heavy** draft-bias.

|---------------------------------|-------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Gift of Proliferation           | A+    | Core scaling; play every turn possible.              |
| Entwined Power                  | A (multiplayer) / B (solo) | Gift to partner for big plays.             |
| Fields Choked with Growth       | B     | Forget candidate T2–T4.                              |
| Sprout Lands into Battleground (or base equivalent) | A- | Terrain-shaping.            |

### Minors to Target

| Card                            | Grade | Why                                                  |
|---------------------------------|-------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Any Plant-bearing 0-cost Minor  | A+    | Threshold support.                                   |
| Gift of Living Energy           | A-    | Energy sustain.                                      |
| Call of the Wilds               | B+    | Plant + movement.                                    |

### Majors that Over-perform

| Card                            | Grade | Why                                                  |
|---------------------------------|-------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Vigor of the Breaking Dawn      | A+    | Plant-heavy; game-ending damage at threshold.        |
| Tigers Hunting                  | A     | Plant + Animal; splat damage.                        |
| Entwined Fates                  | A-    | Fear + spread.                                       |
| Rouse the Trees and Stones      | A-    | Plant-Earth; late closer.                            |

### Avoid drafting

- **Fire-only Majors**. Green has no Fire; threshold failure.
- **Coastal-gated cards**. Green is inland.

## Board Position Evaluation

- **Favorable**: Boards with high jungle+wetland density (A, D).
- **Neutral**: Most boards.
- **Unfavorable**: Mountain-heavy layouts with sparse jungle.

## Game-Phase Strategy

### Early (T1–3)
- Gift of Proliferation every turn.
- Gain Major by T3 (Opening A) or skip (Opening B).
- Plant element accumulation; Level 1 innate prep.

### Mid (T4–6)
- Level 2 innate firing.
- First Major in rotation.
- Second Major consideration T5–T6.
- Fear pool at Terror 2.

### Late (T7+)
- Major chain closes Cities.
- Terror 2 → 3 transition via accumulated Major-kill fear.

## Synergy Partners (Multiplayer)

```admonish tip title="Best Partners"
- **Thunderspeaker** — Entwined Power gifts to Thunderspeaker's dahan-fear engine; double-scaling.
- **Ocean's Hungry Grasp** — inland Green + coastal Ocean = full-board coverage.
- **Shadows Flicker Like Flame** — Green handles board; Shadows fear-rushes; Major synergies.
- **Sharp Fangs Behind the Leaves** — jungle territorial compatibility; both defend inland.
```

```admonish warning title="Anti-Synergy"
- **Heart of the Wildfire** — Wildfire adds blight; Green removes it. Territory split needed or it's frustrating both ways.
- **Vengeance as a Burning Plague** — Vengeance wants blight; Green removes it. Coordinate.
```

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Gaining a Major without Plant-threshold capability. Vigor of the Breaking Dawn at 1 Plant is dead weight.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Playing Entwined Power on yourself in multiplayer. It's a partner-gift card; use it for partner Major plays.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Spreading too wide. Green's innates reward 3–4 clustered lands, not 7 spread lands.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Forgetting Fields Choked without a Major in offer. The Forget is permanent; if the Major doesn't fit, you've lost a card for nothing.
```

## Major vs. Minor — Green Specifically

**Draft bias**: Major-heavy. The archetype's entry spirit in base.

- T1: Minor if offered; otherwise gain via other growth.
- T2: Major (via G4) — Plant-threshold Major preferred.
- T3: Reclaim + play.
- T4–T5: second Major.
- T6+: third Major or Minor fill.
