## Spirit Overview — Framing

Green is the **sacred-site defender that sacrifices presence to prevent blight**. The spirit's signature move is destroying own presence in a Sacred Site to prevent an entire Ravage or Build — a direct blight-prevention mechanic no other spirit matches.

**One-line fantasy**: the jungle reclaims. What was a ravaged land becomes overgrown; the canopy itself absorbs the blow and regrows next turn.

**The honest complexity signal**: Moderate. Green's mechanics (Choke the Land + Steady Regeneration) aren't complex individually, but the turn-by-turn tradeoff — "do I destroy this presence to prevent the ravage?" — is a high-volume decision that rewards careful tempo reading.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Special Rule: Choke the Land with Green

Whenever Invaders would **Ravage or Build** in a land with your **Sacred Site**, you may prevent it by destroying one of your Presence in that land.

**Strategic implication**: this is Green's core defensive tool. A Sacred Site (2+ presence) gives you a "sacrifice a presence, cancel the action" option for any number of Ravages or Builds — as long as you have presence to spend.

### Special Rule: Steady Regeneration

Destroyed Presence can be restored during Growth — **free on Healthy islands**, or **1 Energy per Presence on Blighted islands**.

**Strategic implication**: Choke the Land self-destructs presence; Steady Regeneration is the refill. On a Healthy island, presence-destroy is nearly costless. Once the island becomes Blighted, the cost ramps — which is why Green is best *before* blight cascades, not after.

### Innate: Creepers Tear into Mortar — Slow, 0 Range, Any land

- **Level 1** (1 Moon, 2 Plant): 1 damage to 1 Town/City.
- **Level 2** (2 Moon, 3 Plant): Repeat this Power.
- **Level 3** (3 Moon, 4 Plant): Repeat this Power again.

**Strategic implication**: Level 2+ is the damage engine — repeating Creepers fires 2–3 instances of the damage across turns. This is Green's primary Town/City destroyer.

### Innate: All-Enveloping Green — Fast, 1 Range from Sacred Site, Any land

- **Level 1** (1 Water, 3 Plant): Defend 2.
- **Level 2** (2 Water, 4 Plant): Defend 4 instead.
- **Level 3** (3 Water, 1 Earth, 5 Plant): Defend 4 and remove 1 Blight.

**Strategic implication**: Green's second innate is fast defend scaling into blight removal. Level 3 is the late-game "I remove a blight" trigger.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Sacred Sites are your defensive toolkit.** 2 presence per land unlocks Choke the Land. 2–3 sacred sites by T4 is the target.
2. **Plant is the primary element.** Both innates scale with Plant. Moon + Water + Earth are secondary but required at higher levels.
3. **Destroy presence freely on Healthy islands.** The Steady Regeneration cost is 0 until blight cascades. Green trades presence for action-prevention aggressively pre-blight.
4. **Creepers for Towns/Cities; All-Enveloping for defense.** The two innates do complementary jobs. Don't try to make one do both.
5. **Gift of Proliferation is a multiplayer lever.** In solo it's ok; in 2+ player it enables partners to skip growth-for-presence, freeing them for energy/card gain.
6. **Major Power Shopping is viable.** Green's Plant-heavy element profile enables multi-threshold Majors like Vigor of the Breaking Dawn. Forget-candidate: whichever Unique is weakest for your matchup `[VERIFY: determine which Unique is the canonical Forget target by playtest]`.
7. **Blight cascades flip the math.** Once the island is Blighted, your presence-sacrifice tool costs 1E per Presence to restore. Pre-cascade Green is aggressive; post-cascade Green is defensive.

```admonish tip title="Pro Tip"
Sacred Site placement decisions are higher-leverage for Green than for almost any other spirit. A Sacred Site in a high-Ravage land is a renewable veto; a Sacred Site in a low-Ravage land is wasted. Track the Invader Deck carefully before committing presence-density.
```

## Opening Strategy

### Opening A — Hybrid, Sacred-Site formation 🟥 (needs playtest)

**When to pick this**: default vs. most adversaries. Prioritizes establishing Sacred Sites by T3.

**Target arc**: Sacred Sites in 2 lands by T3 · Level 1 innates firing T3 · Level 2 innate T4–T5 · first Major T4+.

#### Turn 1

- **Growth**: **G3 bottom** (Minor gain + presence placement).
- **Cards played**: one Unique + 0-cost Plant Minor if drafted. Start Sacred Site formation.
- **Presence placement**: inland jungle/wetland; same land as existing presence if possible (sacred-site pairing).
- **Elements by end**: 1 Plant, 1 Moon.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: 1 Sacred Site forming or formed.

#### Turn 2

- **Growth**: **G3 bottom again** (second Minor gain) OR **G2 top** for CP.
- **Cards played**: Unique + drafted Minor. Prep Level 1 innate.
- **Presence placement**: build second sacred-site land.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Plant, 1 Moon, 1 Water.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: 2 Sacred Sites (or 1 Sacred Site + 1 land prepping).

#### Turn 3

- **Growth**: **G1 Reclaim** OR **G4 Major gain** if a Plant-threshold Major is offered.
- **Cards played**: 2–3 cards. Level 1 innate fires (1 Moon + 2 Plant met by Unique + Minor combo `[VERIFY: specific element on Unique]`).
- **Presence placement**: third land.
- **Elements by end**: 3 Plant, 2 Moon, 1 Water.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: Level 2 Creepers Tear into Mortar threshold reached (2 Moon + 3 Plant).

#### Turn 4 — state audit

After T3:

- **Presence**: 5–6 of 13; 2 Sacred Sites.
- **Energy / CP**: 1–2E / 2–3CP.
- **Engine**: Level 2 Creepers firing (repeat damage); Level 1 All-Enveloping Green defending.
- **Fear pool**: 2 of 8 (fear comes from Creepers kills, slower than fear-rush spirits).
- **Blight**: 0 ideally (Choke the Land prevented Ravages).

Pivot advice:
- **Sacred sites slow to form** → G3 bottom one more time; delay Major draft to T5.
- **Island becomes Blighted** → Steady Regeneration cost ramps; shift to All-Enveloping Green defense instead of Choke destruction.
- **Missing Moon element** → next Minor draft must include Moon to unlock Creepers Level 2.

### Strategy Cliff — England L5

```admonish info title="Strategy Cliff — England L5"
**What changes**: buildings gain +1 HP.

**Impact on Green**: Creepers Tear into Mortar Level 1 deals 1 damage to 1 Town/City — at L5, that 1 damage no longer kills a Town (now 2 HP) or a City (now 4 HP). Level 2+ with repeat becomes mandatory.

**Mitigation**: target Level 2 innate reliably by T4 (not T5); consider a Plant-damage Major T3 to supplement Creepers.
```

## Board Position Evaluation

- **Favorable**: jungle + wetland dense boards for Plant-element synergy + Sacred Site terrain.
- **Neutral**: most boards.
- **Unfavorable**: sparse-jungle layouts; Green's innates threshold less reliably.

## Game-Phase Strategy

### Early (T1–3)
- Build 2 Sacred Sites.
- Draft Plant Minors.
- Use Choke the Land liberally (Healthy island = free).

### Mid (T4–6)
- Level 2 Creepers firing (repeat damage).
- First Major in rotation if gained.
- Blight starts mattering; shift toward All-Enveloping Green defense.

### Late (T7+)
- Level 3 All-Enveloping Green removes blight.
- Major Power kills close Cities.
- Terror 2 flip via accumulated kill-fear.

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Destroying presence on a Blighted island without energy. Each Presence restoration costs 1E once Blighted; chained Choke usage on a Blighted island rapidly starves Green of energy.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Placing Sacred Sites in low-Ravage lands. The Sacred Site defensive tool only fires on Ravage/Build. A Sacred Site in an irrelevant land is wasted presence.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Drafting a Plant-less Major. Green's engine is Plant-threshold; a Major without Plant alignment doesn't fit.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Spreading presence across 5+ lands. Sacred Sites need density; 5 lands with 1 presence each = 0 Sacred Sites = 0 Choke usage.
```

## Expansion Sensitivity

- **Base only**: Green's Choke the Land + sacred-site mechanics are fully functional; no expansion dependency for core strategy.
- **+ Branch & Claw**: blight deck introduces per-land blight cascades; Green's Level 3 All-Enveloping Green (blight removal) scales in value.
- **+ Jagged Earth**: deeper Major pool; better Plant-threshold Major options.
- **+ Nature Incarnate + Horizons + Promo Pack 2**: no core-mechanic shift.
