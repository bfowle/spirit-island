## Spirit Overview — Framing

Eyes Watch is a **Defender + Fear-generator + Dahan gatherer** — every Defend Power gathers 1 Dahan, turning stall into counter-attack. Single Fast innate enables pre-Ravage timing.

**Wiki-printed playstyle note**:

> Dahan Trust the Watchers turns Defend effects into Gather effects. Mischief and Sabotage scales into a big Defend + Fear spike.

**Teaching role**: shows that defense is not passive.

## Starting Setup

> Put **2 Presence** on your starting board, in the **highest-numbered Jungle**. You start with your **4 Unique Powers and 0 Energy**.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Special Rule: Dahan Trust the Watchers

> After one of your Powers adds Defend to a single land, Gather up to 1 Dahan into that land.

Every Defend-adding Power also Gathers 1 Dahan — the signature rule.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Moon + Plant drafts.** Mischief's element pressure.
2. **Bottom Track for consistency** — loop Uniques + 1 Minor for sustained Defend + Fear.
3. **Critical draft: 0-cost Moon/Plant Minor** — unlocks Tier 3 every turn in the reclaim loop. Only ~5 such cards exist; ~20% draft odds by third draft.
4. **Every Defend triggers Dahan-gather.** Use it to bait counter-attacks.

## Possible Openings

### Shared starting state

- **2 Presence** on highest Jungle.
- **4 Uniques** in hand.
- **0 Energy**, 1 Card Play.

### Opening A — Bottom Track 🟨 (default)

**T1 · Growth**: G3 bot (+P2, +P0, +1E → 1E).
**T1 · Play**: 0-cost Minor + **Mysterious Abductions**. Taurunti: *"prioritize 0-cost over 1-cost."*

**T2 · Growth**: G2 bot/bot.
**T2 · Play**: Deploy remaining Uniques across T2–T3.

**T3+**: Enter reclaim loop. Target **Mischief L2 (M2/P3 → 1F + Defend 4 + 1 Dahan gathered)** every turn.

### Opening B — Hybrid Majors 🟥

Top-heavy early; grab a Major T3+. Riskier but higher ceiling. Needs partner energy gifts.

### Opening Decision

- **Default Opening A** — reliable.
- **Opening B** when partners provide energy.

## Board / Map Configuration

Per taurunti: prefer boards where every land is within range 1 of a Jungle. Best: **E, B, H**. Worst: **G (poor Jungle distribution) and F**.

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Named mistakes"
1. **Spreading Defend across too many lands in one turn.** Special rule fires per Defend Power; Mischief is "all one Power."
2. **Leaving the starting Jungle undefended.** T1 Ravage there is rare-but-brutal.
3. **Playing Mischief for Defend, ignoring Dahan counterattack.** Gathered Dahan attack in the same land's Ravage resolution.
```
