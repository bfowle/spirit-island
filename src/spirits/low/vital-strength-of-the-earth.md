## Spirit Overview — Framing

Earth is the **canonical Defend & Outlast + Major Power Shopping spirit**. Slow to start, massive to finish. Early turns: defend, survive, bank energy. Late turns: 4–6 cost Majors landing every turn.

**One-line fantasy**: the land itself refuses the invaders. What they destroy regrows; when Majors land, the ravaged land shrugs off blight.

**The honest complexity signal**: Low officially; Earth is where players *learn* Major-Power Shopping. Simple mechanics; execution requires energy-banking discipline. Missing a banking turn means missing a Major T5 which means losing an L5+ game.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Innate: Guardian of the Vital Heart

Earth + Plant + Sun scaling damage. Level 3 among the game's most powerful innates.

### Guard the Healing Land (Unique)

Defend + Dahan-heal. Earth's backbone.

### Gift of Constancy (Unique)

Sustain Earth's defense.

### A Year of Perfect Stillness (Unique)

The game's biggest defend.

### Draw of the Fresh Waters (Unique)

Dahan + Plant; weakest Unique, standard Forget.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Bank energy.** Earth's Majors cost 4–8E. Bank 1–2E per turn T2–T4 to afford T5 Major plays.
2. **Forget Draw of the Fresh Waters.** T2 or T3. Weakest Unique; standard Forget for a Major.
3. **Defend > kill early.** Earth's Uniques are defend-heavy; defend, bank, Major-kill later.
4. **Majors come by T4.** The canonical arc.
5. **Plant + Earth thresholds.** Level 2+ innate.
6. **Stage III will arrive before your best turn.** Plan T6–T7 close around 2–3 Majors in rotation.
7. **Partner coordination in multiplayer.** Earth is the "team dad" — defends inland, buffs dahan, covers weaker partners.

```admonish tip title="Pro Tip"
A Year of Perfect Stillness is a card you play 1–2 times per game. When you play it, ensure the land saved would have been a cascade-blight without it.
```

## Opening Strategy

### Opening A — Slow-ramp, Early Major 🟩

**Target arc**: Major by T4 · Level 2 innate T5 · 3–4 Majors played by T8.

#### Turn 1

- **Growth**: **G2 top** (energy + card play).
- **Cards played**: **Guard the Healing Land + Draw of the Fresh Waters** (spend Draw while you have it).
- **Presence placement**: inland jungle/mountain — first sacred site land.
- **Elements by end**: 1 Earth, 1 Plant.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: 1 ravage defended; 1 dahan saved.

#### Turn 2

- **Growth**: **G1 top** (energy bank).
- **Cards played**: **Guard reclaimed + Gift of Constancy**.
- **Presence placement**: second land, near the first (sacred site building).
- **Elements by end**: 1 Earth, 1 Plant, 1 Sun.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 1E / 2CP (bank).
- **Milestone**: 2E banked; sacred site forming.

#### Turn 3

- **Growth**: **G4 Major gain**. Forget Draw. Gain Plant-Earth Major.
- **Cards played**: **Guard + a Minor or Gift reclaimed**.
- **Presence placement**: third land.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Earth, 2 Plant, 1 Sun.
- **E / CP state**: 2E / 2CP → 1E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: Major in hand; forget complete; energy banked.

#### Turn 4 — state audit

After T3:
- **Presence**: 5–6 of 13; 1 sacred site formed.
- **Energy / CP**: 2–3E / 2–3CP.
- **Engine**: Major in hand; Level 2 innate soon.
- **Fear pool**: 2 of 8 (fear-slow).
- **Blight**: 0–1.

Pivot advice:
- **No appropriate Major offered T3** → hold forget; gain Major T4.
- **Energy-starved** → skip Minor draft; G1 top again.
- **Blight at 2** → Stillness is your emergency valve.

### Opening B — All-defend, late Major 🟨

**When to pick this**: extreme board pressure (Russia L5+, Habsburg Mining L5+).

- **T1–T3**: defend + energy bank. No Major draft.
- **T4**: G4 Major gain; forget Draw.
- **T5**: Major fires.

## Board Position Evaluation

- **Favorable**: Mountain + jungle boards with sacred-site density.
- **Neutral**: Most boards.
- **Unfavorable**: Coastal-heavy, sparse inland.

## Game-Phase Strategy

- **Early (T1–3)**: Guard every turn; bank energy; forget Draw for Major.
- **Mid (T4–6)**: 1–2 Majors in rotation; Level 2 innate firing; second Major gain.
- **Late (T7+)**: 2–3 Majors per turn; Major chain closes cities.

## Synergy Partners (Multiplayer)

```admonish tip title="Best Partners"
- **Lightning** — Lightning damages early; Earth defends + Majors-closes.
- **Shadows** — Shadows fear-rushes; Earth handles board.
- **Sharp Fangs** — Fangs damage + Earth defend.
- **Bringer** — Bringer fear-closes; Earth closes board.
```

```admonish warning title="Anti-Synergy"
- **Keeper / Stone** — both hoard Majors; games go 10+ turns.
```

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Not forgetting Draw of the Fresh Waters. Card stays in hand; you lose Forget slot for a Major.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Not banking energy T1–T3. Can't afford Majors T4–T5.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Playing Stillness on a defendable ravage. Emergency card only.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Picking Earth for fast-scaling adversary (Brandenburg-Prussia L6 Stage III fast). Timing doesn't align.
```

## Major vs. Minor — Earth Specifically

**Draft bias**: Major-heavy. Canonical archetype.

- T1–T2: Minors or skip; bank energy.
- T3–T4: first Major (forget Draw).
- T5–T6: second Major.
- T7+: third Major.
