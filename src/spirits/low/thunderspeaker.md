## Spirit Overview — Framing

Thunderspeaker is the **dahan multiplier**. Every turn, their plays buff, move, or empower the island's native people — turning dahan counterattack from an incidental damage source into the spirit's primary damage engine, their defensive shield, and their fear-output amplifier.

**One-line fantasy**: the voice that rouses the dahan from fear to action; invaders see the island's people stand and push back, and that *is* the terror.

**The honest complexity signal**: Thunderspeaker is printed as Low-complexity, but dahan tracking adds overhead — you're tracking not just invader positions but dahan density in every relevant land, every turn. Players learning Thunderspeaker from base-game intuitions often under-value dahan preservation early, lose their density by T4, and watch the engine collapse. The spirit rewards patience with dahan and aggressive use of dahan once density is established.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Innate: Thunderous Spirits (Manifestation of Power and Glory)

The dahan-density scaler. Thunderspeaker's core innate fires fear + defend effects scaling with dahan in target lands. At low dahan density, the innate produces modest effects; at high density (3+ dahan per land), it produces game-closing turns.

- **Level 1 (1 Sun)**: modest defend + fear.
- **Level 2 (2 Sun, 1 Air)**: meaningful fear scaling; starts carrying its weight.
- **Level 3 (3 Sun, 2 Air, 1 Fire)**: game-ending fear output on dahan-dense lands.

### Special Rule: Dahan empowerment

Several Thunderspeaker powers give dahan +damage on retaliation during ravages. This compounds — a dahan with +1 retaliation in a 3-dahan land does 9 damage instead of 6, enough to kill 1 City or 2 Towns + an Explorer outright.

### Offering of Vigor (Unique)

Fast defend + dahan buff. Your workhorse Fast power; usually played every turn.

### Manifestation of Power and Glory (Unique)

Summons dahan + innate trigger. Part of the "hero" opening sequence in all variants.

### Words of Warning (Unique)

Defend + dahan retaliation buff. Paired with Offering of Vigor as the core defend stack.

### Sudden Ambush (Unique)

Aggressive damage via dahan pre-strike. Powerful but costly; used situationally.

### Voice of Thunder (Unique)

Fear-heavy defend; late-game fear generator.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Dahan density is the engine.** 3+ dahan in 2–3 key lands is the target state by T4. Less density = weaker innate firings; more density = game-ending.
2. **Defend aggressively in dahan lands.** Every dahan lost is future retaliation damage lost. Offering of Vigor + Words of Warning stacks defend to preserve population.
3. **Summon early (when possible).** Manifestation of Power and Glory places a dahan; that dahan retaliates next Ravage; that retaliation kills a Town and generates fear. Compounding starts on T1–T2 if played correctly.
4. **Sun element is load-bearing.** All three innate levels require Sun. Draft Sun-element Minors aggressively; without Sun, the spirit is slow.
5. **Avoid over-extension.** Dahan-Rush wants 2–3 focus lands, not spread presence across 6+. Concentrate; don't dilute.
6. **Major Powers enter the picture T5+.** Terrifying Nightmares and Voice of Command (both fear-heavy Majors) fit this spirit's Sun/Air/Fire profile. Don't rush Majors before dahan density is set.
7. **Dahan-hostile adversaries demand pivot.** Against France (Plantation Colony) or Russia (Settlers), dahan-preservation is actively fought by the adversary. Shift archetype toward Fear-Rush or bring a partner that handles those adversary-specific threats.

```admonish tip title="Pro Tip"
Before Slow powers, count dahan in each projected Ravage land. If the total dahan damage + your Slow-phase damage won't kill all invaders in a land, the dahan die next turn; pivot to Fast-phase kills or defend harder.
```

## Opening Strategy

Thunderspeaker has three well-documented opening variants via latentoctopus. Each serves a different adversary and tempo need.

### Opening A — Hybrid, Minor-heavy 🟨

**Source**: [latentoctopus — Thunderspeaker Opening 1](https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/thunderspeaker-opening1/)

**When to pick this**: default against most base and B&C adversaries. Good energy balance and dahan density ramp.

**Target arc**: 3 CP by T4 · Level 2 innate firing by T5 · Terror 2 flip by T6–T7.

#### Turn 1

- **Growth**: **G3 bottom** (place presence + gain card play).
- **Cards played**: **Sudden Ambush + Manifestation of Power and Glory** — preserves targets for turn 2 (Manifestation summons a dahan that's then used for retaliation) OR **Manifestation + Voice of Thunder** if you want to unlock Level 2 earlier.
- **Presence placement**: inland jungle land with existing dahan; aim for density clustering.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Sun, 1 Air, 1 Fire (depending on card selection).
- **E / CP state**: enter 1E / 2CP → exit 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: 1–2 dahan summoned into the target land; Level 1 innate fired.

#### Turn 2

- **Growth**: **G2 top** (gain energy, presence top track).
- **Cards played**: **Words of Warning + a Minor** — Words buffs the dahan retaliation for T2 ravage; Minor is ideally a Sun-element card.
- **Presence placement**: adjacent to T1 land; maintain clustering.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Sun maintained, 2 Air now present.
- **E / CP state**: enter 1E / 2CP → exit 0–1E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: dahan retaliation kills a Town during Ravage; 1 fear generated from the kill + innate fear.

#### Turn 3

- **Growth**: **G1 reclaim + G3 bottom** (Reclaim refreshes hand; gain 2 Minor Powers).
- **Cards played**: **Offering of Vigor + the better of your 2 newly-drafted Minors**. Target elemental coverage.
- **Presence placement**: one more land — if coastal pressure mounting, shift coastal; else extend inland coverage.
- **Elements by end**: 3 Sun, 2 Air, 1 Fire approaching Level 2 threshold.
- **E / CP state**: enter 1E / 2CP → exit 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: 2–3 dahan density in 2 key lands; hand has 3 Minors + 2–3 Uniques.

#### Turn 4 — state audit

After T3 you should have:

- **Presence on board**: 6–7 of 13, clustered across 3 lands.
- **Energy / CP**: 1E / 2CP, possibly 2E / 3CP depending on growth.
- **Engine state**: Level 2 innate (2 Sun, 1 Air) firing reliably; dahan count on critical lands at 3+.
- **Fear pool**: 3–4 of 8 — on track for Terror 2 by T6.
- **Blight**: 0–1.
- **Hand**: 4–5 cards (after Reclaim + Minors).

Pivot advice:

- **Missing Sun element** → draft Sun-Minor on T4. Delay Level 2 innate firing to T5.
- **Dahan dying too fast** → shift to Offering of Vigor + Words of Warning every turn; accept lower fear output for preservation.
- **Inland cluster isolated from invaders** → use Sudden Ambush to force push/pull of nearby invaders into the cluster.

### Opening B — Energy-focused 🟨

**Source**: [latentoctopus — Thunderspeaker Opening 2](https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/thunderspeaker-opening2/)

**When to pick this**: slow-adversary matchups (England at L0–L3) where late scaling pays off. Prioritizes energy accumulation at the cost of early presence spread.

**Target arc**: 3 CP by T4 with energy bank for T5 Major play.

#### Turn 1

- **Growth**: **G3 bottom** (energy-heavy growth option + Minor gain).
- **Cards played**: **Manifestation + a Minor** — summon one dahan, draft a Minor toward Sun/Air element coverage.
- **Presence placement**: inland, plant-dahan adjacent.
- **Elements by end**: 1–2 Sun, 1 Air.
- **E / CP state**: enter 1E / 2CP → exit 1E / 2CP (energy bank).
- **Milestone**: 1 dahan summoned; 1E banked for T2 Words of Warning.

#### Turn 2

- **Growth**: **G3 bottom again** (energy + card gain; sustain the bank).
- **Cards played**: **Words of Warning + second Minor**.
- **Presence placement**: second land, nearby.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Sun, 2 Air.
- **E / CP state**: enter 2E / 2CP → exit 1E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: dahan retaliation fires T2 Ravage with +1 damage bonus; 2–3 fear generated total.

#### Turn 3

- **Growth**: **G1 Reclaim + G2 top** (refresh hand; energy top-slot).
- **Cards played**: **Offering of Vigor + Sudden Ambush** (or reclaimed Manifestation).
- **Presence placement**: third land.
- **Elements by end**: 3 Sun, 2 Air.
- **E / CP state**: enter 2E / 2CP → exit 1E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: 3 CP next turn; Level 2 innate available; 2E banked.

#### Turn 4 — state audit

Opening B state (vs. Opening A):

- **Presence**: 5–6 on board (tighter cluster than A).
- **Energy / CP**: 2E / 2–3CP; a planned Major gain is sustainable.
- **Engine**: Level 2 innate available; dahan density 2–3 per land.
- **Fear pool**: 3 of 8.

Pivot advice:
- Same as Opening A, but with the extra energy available for emergency Offerings.
- This opening is less resilient vs. early adversary spikes (Sweden's fear penalties hit harder here).

### Opening C — Late-Game Scaling 🟨

**Source**: [latentoctopus — Thunderspeaker Opening 3](https://latentoctopus.github.io/guide/thunderspeaker-opening3/)

**When to pick this**: slow adversaries (classic: England at higher levels) where a T5+ engine pays off but T1–T2 are manageable.

**Target arc**: 4 Air + 4 Sun by T5; 3 CP; late Terror 3 via fear-heavy Major.

#### Turn 1

- **Growth**: **G2 top** (energy-top; delay presence placement).
- **Cards played**: **Sudden Ambush OR Voice of Thunder** — one of the two, not both. Target innate threshold over spread.
- **Presence placement**: one inland land.
- **Elements by end**: 1 Sun, 1 Air.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: weak T1 (intentional); 1 invader damaged or 1 dahan summoned.

#### Turn 2

- **Growth**: **G2 top again** (keep energy ramp).
- **Cards played**: **Words of Warning + 1 Minor**.
- **Presence placement**: second land.
- **Elements by end**: 2 Sun, 1 Air.
- **E / CP state**: 1E / 2CP → 0E / 2CP.
- **Milestone**: still weak but curve is ramping; dahan count 1–2 on engaged lands.

#### Turn 3

- **Growth**: **G3 bottom** (finally place presence + gain Minor).
- **Cards played**: **Offering + Manifestation + remaining**.
- **Presence placement**: third land.
- **Elements by end**: 3 Sun, 2 Air.
- **E / CP state**: 2E / 3CP → 0–1E / 3CP (CP unlocked).
- **Milestone**: **3 CP unlocked**; 3 dahan across key lands.

#### Turn 4 — state audit

Opening C state:

- **Presence**: 6 on board; slightly less spread than A.
- **Energy / CP**: 1–2E / 3CP.
- **Engine**: Level 2 innate active; targets full Level 3 (4 Sun, 4 Air) by T5.
- **Fear pool**: 2–3 of 8 (slower fear than A).
- **Blight**: 0.

Pivot advice:
- If T1–T2 feel terrible, that's expected — don't panic. The engine arrives T4–T5.
- Don't draft Majors before T5; Opening C's energy bank is the Major budget.

## Element & Aspect Preferences

**Preferred elements**: Sun (primary — all innate levels), Air (secondary — Level 2+), Fire (tertiary — Level 3 only). Draft Sun-first; Air-second; skip Fire-only cards unless threshold-reach is clear.

**Aspects**:

- **Warrior** — more aggressive; dahan damage + mobility shifts toward direct confrontation. Strong vs. slow adversaries; weaker vs. dahan-hostile adversaries.
- **Tactician** — supports more complex dahan positioning; scaling via specific land targeting. Good for experienced Thunderspeaker players.
- **Mentor** — buffs dahan scaling via Sun-element innates. Strong multiplayer pick when paired with a dahan-neutral partner.

**Pick default**: base Thunderspeaker for first games; Warrior once comfortable; Mentor in multiplayer with a fear-heavy partner.

| Card                            | Grade | Why                                                        |
|---------------------------------|-------|------------------------------------------------------------|

### Cards to AVOID drafting

- **Blight-positive Majors** (e.g., cards that damage via blight). Thunderspeaker wants dahan alive; blight-cascade kills dahan.
- **Range-0-only cards that don't match your presence clusters**. Needs mobility; avoid immobile effects.

## Board Position Evaluation

- **Favorable**: Boards with dense inland jungle clusters (A, D, F). Dahan start here; density ramps naturally.
- **Neutral**: Standard base boards.
- **Unfavorable**: Board E (too spread out; dahan density harder to compound). Coastal-heavy boards where dahan are at coasts — drowning interactions get weird with Ocean partners.

## Game-Phase Strategy

### Early (T1–3)

- Summon dahan via Manifestation every chance available.
- Defend hard in any land with dahan + invaders.
- Target 2–3 clustered lands; ignore spread opportunities.
- Draft Sun + Air Minors exclusively.

### Mid (T4–6)

- Level 2 innate firing each turn.
- Dahan density 3+ per key land; sustained via defend.
- Transition: gain a Major T5 if Sun-Major offered.
- Fear pool should hit Terror 2 by T6.

### Late (T7+)

- Terror 3 flip achievable via Major + innate combo.
- Dahan retaliation closes Cities.
- Don't grow new presence — existing density is the engine.

## Synergy Partners (Multiplayer)

```admonish tip title="Best Partners"
- **Sharp Fangs Behind the Leaves** — dahan + beasts share battlefield; double retaliation stacks.
- **Shadows Flicker Like Flame** — Thunderspeaker handles board; Shadows fear-rushes. Classic pairing.
- **Bringer of Dreams and Nightmares** — Bringer's fear cards scale with dahan; Thunderspeaker feeds dahan density.
- **Vital Strength of the Earth** — Earth defends; Thunderspeaker scales kills via dahan retaliation.
```

```admonish warning title="Anti-Synergy / Territory Conflicts"
- **Vengeance as a Burning Plague** — Vengeance wants blight; Thunderspeaker needs dahan alive (blight kills dahan). Territory split required.
- **Heart of the Wildfire** — similar blight-conflict.
- **Volcano Looming High** — destruction kills dahan unconditionally.
```

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Letting dahan die to T1–T2 ravages "to save presence." Dahan are the engine; lost dahan destroy mid-game scaling. Defend them, even at presence cost.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Spreading presence across 5+ lands. Dahan-Rush wants clustering; 5 lands with 1 dahan each is 5 kills of 2 damage, not 1 kill of 10. Concentrate.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Drafting Air-only Minors over Sun-bearing ones. Sun is load-bearing; Air is support. Sun-first.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Playing Sudden Ambush T1 when Manifestation would summon a dahan. Ambush uses existing dahan; Manifestation grows them. Growth first.
```

```admonish failure title="Common Mistake"
Playing Thunderspeaker against France or Russia without pivoting. The entire engine fights the adversary's mechanics. Switch spirits or archetype.
```

## Major vs. Minor — Thunderspeaker Specifically

**Draft bias**: Mixed (Minor-heavy T1–T3; Major T5+).

- T1–T3: Minors only. Sun-bearing auto-take. Air-Sun dual-element = gold.
- T4–T5: first Major gain. Forget Voice of Thunder if a Sun-Major lands; otherwise forget an early-drafted Minor.
- T6+: second Major optional; sometimes more Minors for fear-rush finish.

See [Major vs. Minor Fundamentals](../../fundamentals/major-vs-minor.md).
