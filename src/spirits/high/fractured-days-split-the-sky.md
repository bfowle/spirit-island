## Spirit Overview — Framing

Fractured Days is **the game's most decision-heavy spirit** — Time tokens as shared currency, Days That Never Were as a private major/minor preview pool, and Slip the Flow as a partner reclaim/card-play timing manipulator. jyonker13:

> It's mighty difficult to be prescriptive… this Opening Thread is going to suggest an appropriately 'fractured' approach.

**Non-obvious traits**:

- **Irregular reclaim cadence** — doesn't reclaim on a 3–4 turn beat.
- **Days That Never Were is a telegraph** — seeing a Major T1 lets you build Minor drafts around it. jyonker13: *"absolutely massive value… the crux of your decision making."*
- **Starter tier**: Blur the Arc > Absolute Stasis > The Past Returns Again > Pour Time Sideways.

**Complexity signal**: Very High. Fractured Days is widely considered the deepest moderate-complexity spirit and tips into "high" on play load.

## Core Mechanics & Special Rules

### Special Rule: Fragments of Shattered Time

> Each Presence on this ability represents 1 Time. Many of your Powers require Time as an additional cost. Spend it when you Resolve the Power. When you Gain 1 Time, put 1 of your Presence here from your Presence track (or, optionally, the island). When you Spend 1 Time, return it to a Presence track — or if you have no free spaces, Destroy it.

**Time = Presence**. Every Time spent is a presence used. This is why Fractured is fragile — unlike all other spirits, playing Powers can literally destroy your presence if tracks are full.

### Special Rule: Days That Never Were

> Starts with 4 face-up cards visible; you can gain cards from this preview pool via G3 or specific effects.

## Key Strategic Principles

1. **Blur the Arc of Years is the fulcrum.** Never forget — carries all three innate elements.
2. **Days That Never Were telegraphs Majors T1.** Build Minor drafts around them.
3. **Time = Presence = scarce.** Every Power that costs Time is a presence-spend decision.
4. **Irregular reclaim cadence.** No 3–4 turn beat.
5. **Forget Pour Time Sideways by default** — unless multi-board coverage requires it.
6. **Slip the Flow self-target on T2** fast-activates Visions of a Shifting Future.

## Possible Openings

### Shared starting state

- **3 Presence** on starting board (1 Dahan-land + 2 Dahan-less land).
- **4 Uniques in hand**: Blur the Arc, Absolute Stasis, Pour Time Sideways, The Past Returns Again.
- **4 cards visible** in Days That Never Were.
- **Starting income**: 1 Energy, 1 Card Play.

### Opening A — jyonker13's branching canonical 🟨

**T1 · Growth**: G3 (Gain Sun + Move presence + DTNW-card-gain).
- Gain 3 Time (2 from energy, 1 from plays).

**T1 · Play**: 1 Minor (pick for *effect*, not elements — even a push-Explorer effect is fine). Move presence either to enable the Minor or seed sacred-site adjacency for Absolute Stasis T2.

**T2 · Growth**: G2 (Gain Moon + Minor + presence from energy + 2 card plays + 1 Time).
**T2 · Play**: **Absolute Stasis** (1 Time — a land doesn't exist this turn), **Blur the Arc** (Dahan creation/movement), **The Past Returns Again** (for elements).
- Self-target Slip the Flow to fast-activate Visions of a Shifting Future → preview next-turn Event OR bottom-deck Farmers Seek the Dahan / Outpaced.
- Then **reclaim one played card** based on T3 plan.

**T3 · Growth (branch picker)** — five paths per jyonker13:
1. **Major-rush**: G3 + gain Major from DTNW, Forget Pour Time or lesser Minor.
2. **Reclaim-enabler for partner**: G3 + 3 Time; Moon Minor + Past Returns to cancel first Stage-II Escalation (5P game — everyone chips 1E).
3. **Slow-fast converter**: G2 or G3 + Moon Minor + Blur → Slip the Flow L1/L2.
4. **Extra-card-play gift**: G3 + Sun/Air + Stasis → Slip the Flow L3.
5. **Fallback**: G2 + 2 Time from energy + Moon Minor → Slip L1 + Pour Time.

**T4**: Depends on T3 branch; one more turn before Reclaim All.

### Opening B — Solo 🟥

About half the time take a **Major from DTNW on T1** instead of a Minor (solo spends less Time). Forget Pour Time — *"that's what it's there for."*

**T2**: Gain strongest Minor; Reclaim Major-or-Stasis depending on overlap.
**T3**: Play the Major if reclaimed; else dig via growth for Minor + Stasis combo.

### Opening Decision

- **Default Opening A** — jyonker13's branching canonical.
- **Opening B (solo)** in solo with DTNW Major telegraphed.

## Game-Phase Strategy

### Early (T1–3)
- T1 Minor + DTNW preview.
- T2 Stasis + Blur + Past Returns; Slip self-target.
- T3 branch-pick based on board + DTNW Majors.

### Mid (T4–6)
- Major integration via DTNW.
- Reclaim-cadence tuned to Time-management.
- Slip the Flow L2 for partner Reclaim-gifts.

### Late (T7+)
- Visions L2 for deck-manipulation (bottom-deck bad Events).
- Major-stack turns with Time burst.

## Synergy Partners (Multiplayer)

- **Slow-heavy partners** — Slip L1 Fast-converts their Slows.
- **Card-draft-heavy partners** — Slip L2 enables double-plays.
- **Extra-CP partners** — Slip L3 gives +1 card play.
- **Avoid**: spirits with rigid reclaim cadences.

## Common Mistakes

```admonish failure title="jyonker13 named mistakes"
1. **Using Time to repeat Blur the Arc T2.** Wasted cost; save Time for Slip-the-Flow repeats or Stasis.
2. **Picking T1/T2 Minors by element rather than by effect** when no Major is previewed.
3. **Forgetting Blur the Arc** — user 2052317's warning: it's the Moon card; forgetting cripples innate access.
4. **Playing Pour Time Sideways without having seen the Event.** Risks amplifying a nasty Event.
5. **Not reclaiming something on T2.** Branching T3 framework assumes you reclaimed Blur/Stasis/Past-Returns.
```
