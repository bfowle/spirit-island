# Reader Self-Assessment

Three questions. Your answers route you to the right chapters to read first. The book is big; most readers only need 20–30 pages for their current level.

## Question 1 — How many Spirit Island games have you played?

**0–2 games**:
- Start at [The Core Loop](./foundations/core-loop.md) and [Terminology](./foundations/terminology.md).
- Read [How to Use This Book](./how-to-use.md).
- Skip Parts II–VIII for now; you need the foundation first.
- Play 5 more games. Then come back.

**3–10 games**:
- You know the rules. Read [Tempo](./fundamentals/tempo.md), [Major vs. Minor](./fundamentals/major-vs-minor.md), [Presence Economy](./fundamentals/presence-economy.md).
- Pick one spirit chapter (Brett-priority: Shadows, Thunderspeaker, Ocean, Green, Bringer). Read it before your next game with that spirit.
- Skim [Teaching Methods](./social/teaching-methods.md) if you'll be teaching someone.

**10–50 games**:
- Read Parts II, IV (adversaries you've played), VI (archetypes), VII (social).
- Pick your next spirit-to-master (Brett priorities) and read that chapter.
- Start using the [Claude skills](./appendices/claude-skills-guide.md) for daily challenge loop.

**50+ games**:
- The book's stats + source-citations are for you. Read [Statistics Lens](./statistics/index.md) *(stub)* when authored.
- Audit your playlog; identify weakest spirit/adversary; read that chapter.
- Consider contributing revisions — you have table experience the chapters don't yet capture.

## Question 2 — What's your current play format?

**True solo (1 spirit, 1 hand)**:
- [Solo strategy is woven through every spirit chapter](./spirits/index.md).
- [Fear Track](./fundamentals/fear-track.md) and [Blight Track](./fundamentals/blight-track.md) apply solo.
- Skip Part VII (social) until you go multiplayer.

**Multi-handed (1 player, 2+ spirits)**:
- Same spirit chapters; read multiple.
- [Archetype Index](./combos/archetype-index.md) for partner-spirit selection.
- [Two-Player Dynamics](./social/two-player.md) — the "multi-handed tip" at the end of that chapter applies here.

**Real multiplayer (2–4 players)**:
- [Part VII](./social/index.md) in full.
- Specifically [Alpha-Player Problem](./social/alpha-player-problem.md) and [Mixed-Skill Tables](./social/mixed-skill-tables.md).
- [Archetype Index](./combos/archetype-index.md) for coverage planning.

**Convention / meetup**:
- [Convention & Meetup Norms](./social/convention-and-meetup-norms.md).
- [Playing with Strangers](./social/playing-with-strangers.md).
- [Teaching Methods](./social/teaching-methods.md) if teaching.

## Question 3 — What's your current win rate at L3?

**Under 50%**:
- Your gap is likely tempo or draft. Read [Tempo](./fundamentals/tempo.md) and [Major vs. Minor](./fundamentals/major-vs-minor.md).
- You're likely drafting cards that don't fit your spirit's archetype.
- Log 10 games via [`si-post-game`](./appendices/claude-skills-guide.md); the common-mistake surfacing will diagnose specific issues.

**50–75%**:
- You have fundamentals. Your gap is likely matchup-specific.
- Read the adversary chapter for your worst-matchup.
- Try unfamiliar spirits (Brett priorities) to broaden exposure.

**75%+**:
- You're ready for L5+ and scenarios.
- Read the archetype chapters and start pairing spirits deliberately.
- Explore aspects (coming in M3+).

## What this book won't tell you

- **Rules you don't know**: read the rulebook or [Querki FAQ](https://querki.net/u/darker/spirit-island-faq/) first. This book assumes game literacy.
- **How to lose gracefully**: well, kind of — see [Post-Game Debrief](./social/post-game-debrief.md). But losing is an internal skill.
- **Which spirit is "best"**: meta shifts; every spirit wins at L6 with the right partner and adversary. "Best" is context.

## If you get stuck

- Re-run the daily-challenge skill.
- Revisit your most-lost matchup in the adversary chapter.
- Post a specific question in the [Spirit of the Island Discord](https://discord.com/invite/spirit-of-the-island-676737342123278337).
- Update the book's changelog with what you learned.
