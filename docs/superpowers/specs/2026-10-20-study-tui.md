# si study: the hindsight review as a terminal board

Status: design and first build 2026-10-20. Brett: "how do we go ahead with the TUI
version of this. I would like it to then be possible in the TUI to go turn by turn,
evaluating, with any possible ratatui-based graphs that would help understand the
pacing and such, as well as being able to load that checkpointed state in-game as we
did with `inject` directly." Follows the hindsight spec section 6 (study board) and
the companion direction: one screen per question, Brett's yes before the next.

## 1. What it is

`si study <game>` opens the same review `si hindsight` prints, as screens to move
through instead of a report to scroll. Same engine, same numbers: `si-hindsight` runs
in a worker thread turn by turn and each turn lands on the screen as it finishes;
the record (board counts, Fear, Energy, cards per turn) is drawn at once, before any
review is in. ratatui 0.30 and crossterm 0.29, the versions adroit uses; no second
TUI stack in the house.

## 2. Screens

**Turn** (the question: what was the play here, and what else was open?)

Nine panels on one screen, so the position, the resources and the lines sit side by
side instead of the lines table alone over empty rows (Brett, 2026-10-21: "we have so
much whitespace for the selection itself; we need other panels showing all the info
around growth/cards/choices... lands, events/fear/invader stuff"). On a terminal 150
columns or wider: the decision down the left column (LINES, READING, LINE), the island
down the right (POSITION, INVADER PHASE, FORECAST), the spirit's resources across the
bottom (SPIRIT, GROWTH, HAND) at the height their content needs. Narrower, the same
panels stack in rows with LINES keeping at least a third of the height. Every panel is
built for its width before the layout is cut, so text panels are as tall as their lines
and tables as tall as their wrapped rows.

- a tab bar across the top on both screens: every turn as a folder tab drawn with the
  panels' rounded corners (`╭ T4 MISTAKE 1.7 ╮`), its verdict colored (sound green,
  better yellow, mistake red, unreviewed dim), the current turn open into the screen
  below and the others closed by the baseline; the two screens, Turn and Pacing, as
  tabs at the right end. Labels shrink to `T4 1.7`, then `T4`, when the bar would
  overflow. ratatui's own `Tabs` widget is titles with a divider, which read as one
  run of text (Brett, 2026-10-21: "the top area kind of runs together; can we make the
  turn tabs more tab-like?"); powerline shapes would need a Nerd font, so box-drawing
  it is. `h`/`l` or the arrows move, `1`-`9` jump, `Tab` switches screens;
- **LINES** (top left): the lines as a tree (Brett: "the same growth/cards can expand
  out to a tree view for different targeting instead of all individual lines"): growth,
  then the play set by card names, then each targeting with its lands; a group row
  shows its best member's numbers and a count, Enter opens it, `e`/`c` open or close
  all; the first growth (the best) starts open to its play sets until the tree is
  touched. Columns: tag, growth, cards, innates, informed, hindsight, conf, tiers, produced;
  the elements moved to the LINE panel. `j`/`k` move the cursor, text wraps in its cell.
  Under it **READING**: the sentence, the decision and luck numbers, the gap split into
  plays and growth, what the hidden cards were worth against the expectation;
- **POSITION** (right column): the island as the Fast phase began, one row per land:
  terrain (`Sa c` = coastal Sands), Invaders, Dahan, Blight, presence, tokens; `steps`
  = the Invader cards reaching the land this turn (`R`, `B`, `HI` where Invaders stand,
  lowercase where the terrain matches an empty land) and the land's Explore chance
  (`X50%`); `line` = the selected row's plays aimed there and its presence placements
  (`+Pr`), the row lit yellow; `result` = what the steps did there as recorded
  (`R:Bl1 Dh2`, `B:+T`, `X:+E`);
- **INVADER PHASE** (under POSITION): the face-up Ravage and Build cards with the lands
  they act in and the result in each; the Explore as face down with its pool, then the
  card that came; the Event and the Fear cards resolved, each choice they asked with
  its verdict; the Blight card's pool and the Blight added; the board after;
- **FORECAST** (under INVADER PHASE): the deck per coming turn (stage, pool, expected
  re-hits and fresh lands, each land's chance);
- **SPIRIT**, **GROWTH**, **HAND** (bottom band): Energy, income, plays, the track's
  elements and presence; each innate's tiers as element thresholds with the ones the
  selected row reaches lit (Brett, 2026-10-21: elements always as the eight in printed
  order with every count, zeros included, each in its color: `Su1 Mo3 Fi1 Ai0 Wa1 Ea0
  Pl5 An0`, the palette the printed report uses); the panel's growth groups in yield words with the one
  taken and the best line's marked, the draft offered and the card taken, Reclaim and
  Forget; the hand at the Fast phase with cost, speed, range, target and elements, and
  whether the actual line, the selected row or both play each card;
- **LINE** (under READING): the selected row in words (notation, growth, elements and
  tiers, produced, scores, the Slow lands chosen after the Invader steps, notes; a note
  the simulator raised in several lands or for several effects is shown once);
- `t` opens a prompt for a line in notation (`SLSF>C8 MI`), scored with `try_line`
  and shown as a `try` row; `i` loads this turn's start-of-turn checkpoint into the
  running game (section 4); `?` the key.

**Pacing** (the question: how did the game move, turn by turn?)

Charts over the turns, from the record and the review:

- Blight on the island and Blight added per turn, with the Blight card's pool where the
  capture kept the save (the loss clock);
- Invaders on the island: Explorers, Towns, Cities after each turn, and lands with
  Invaders (the breadth of the pressure);
- Fear: cards earned per turn and cumulative, the Terror Level steps (the win clock);
- the spirit: Energy after the turn, plays, cards played, presence on the island (Rei's
  scaling curve);
- the review: the actual line's informed and hindsight per turn, the decision gap and
  the luck per turn as bars (where the game was lost on what was knowable, and where
  the cards took it).

Block and half-block markers only; no braille, dots or circles (Brett's terminal).

`Tab` switches screens; `q` quits; the worker's progress (turn, positions tried) sits
in the footer while the review runs.

## 3. Engine changes

`review_turns(rec, defs, opts, only: Option<&[u32]>, on_progress)` reviews the named
turns and reports progress through a callback (`Progress::{Positions, Ranking, Done}`)
instead of printing; `review_with` is the same over every turn with no callback, and
`si hindsight` prints its bars from the callback. The TUI reviews one turn per call in
a worker thread and streams the `TurnReview`s over a channel.

## 4. Into the game

`i` on a turn runs `si inject <capture> --turn n --phase SpiritPhase` as a child
process (the binary itself) and shows what it printed in a panel: the same path Brett
used by hand, the checkpoint on the path the game finally took. A bare EndGame log has
no checkpoints; the panel says so and names `si import`. Once the branch is played
out, `si study <its EndGame log> --seed <that save>` reviews the branch.

## 5. Later

The board itself drawn from the recorded snapshots (`before_fast`, `before_invaders`,
`before_slow`) with the selected line's pieces moved on it; picking a target on the
board instead of typing notation; several spirits.
