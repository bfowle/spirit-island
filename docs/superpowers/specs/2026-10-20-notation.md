# Spirit Island notation (SIN)

Status: design 2026-10-20, v0.1; the emitter (`si notate`) renders any recorded game
in it. Brett's ask: "chess has a way of writing down all the moves, go too; should we
come up with a nomenclature for SI, in order to replay / reroll things?"

Yes. Chess notation records the decisions and lets the position be rebuilt; the
result glyphs (`+`, `#`) and verdicts (`!`, `?`) are annotations on top. Spirit Island
has two players' worth of moves, the Spirits' and the island's, and the island's are
reveals the players did not choose. The notation keeps the three apart:

| Kind | Who | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **decision** | the player | growth group and slots, where presence went, cards played, each Power's target and its inner choices |
| **reveal** | the decks | the Invader card, the Event, the Fear cards, the four Power cards offered at a gain |
| **result** | the rules | Blight added, Dahan lost, pieces destroyed, Fear generated; always in brackets, always derivable |

A line of play is decisions plus reveals. Rebuilding the board needs nothing else;
results are there for the human reader and for checking the rebuild. A **reroll**
replays the decisions with one reveal changed; a **variation** replays the reveals
with one decision changed. Both are written in the same notation, in parentheses
after the move they replace, as chess writes variations.

## 1. Vocabulary

Lands: board letter and number (`C2`, thematic `NW3`). Pieces: `E T C D` for
Explorer, Town, City, Dahan; `Pr` presence (with a spirit index `Pr2` when more
than one spirit); `Bl` Blight. Tokens: `Di` Disease, `St` Strife, `Wi` Wilds, `Be`
Beast, `Ba` Badlands, `Is` Isolate. Energy: `3E`. Cards and innates: a short code
from the title's initials with stopwords dropped (`SLSF` Strike Low with Sudden
Fevers, `FV` Fiery Vengeance, `PLAG` Plaguebearers, `ERR` Epidemics Run Rampant);
collisions take a digit; every game's notation ends with its legend. Invader cards
by stage and terrain (`Sands1`, `MountainJungle3`), `^` for an escalation card.

Spirit panel slots: `e2` the second Energy-track slot, `c3` the third Card-Plays
slot (the engine's `Energy2`, `CardPlays3`).

## 2. A turn

```
T3
  G  G2 c4@C8 c5@C6                       growth: group, slots revealed and where each presence went
  P  SLSF FV  (2E)                        cards played and the Energy paid; E=1 after
  F  SLSF>C5[F+1 ×R]  FV>C6[dmg2 T†]      Fast: Power>target, results in brackets
  I  Ev ThrivingTrade[C2 +C] · Fr SeekSafety TallTales · HI Sands1[C2 +C] · R Wetland1[C8 Bl Pr† D†] · B Jungle1[C1 +T  C4 +C] · X Mountain2[C3 +E  C7 +E]
  S  PLAG>C5[⇒4 →C2 ×2]  SR>C2[dmg2]       Slow
  TP  Bl=4 Fr+2 TL1                        Time Passes: Blight on the island, Fear cards earned this turn, Terror Level
```

Growth tokens: `G3` group; `e2@C2` slot revealed and the land the presence took;
`+Pr@C5` presence from elsewhere (a card, a rule); `Rc` Reclaim (`Rc1:SLSF` one
card); `+SDIW` card gained (`+SDIW<CTF GLE SHSR>` with the three not taken, when
known); `-FV` card forgotten; `+2E` Energy from the growth. In a hindsight
variation, `(draft T4)` after a gained card says the draft was taken from a later
turn's recorded offer (the deck order was fixed, so a gain here would have shown it).

Plays in the hindsight report: cards first, innates after `| innate` (`SLSF>C2 FV>C4 |
innate ERR>C2 SR>C1`; underlined in color), so a Power Card and an innate never read
alike. Elements in short form: `Su1 Mo1 Fi2 Ai1 Wa1 Ea1 Pl1 An2`. Several spirits: each line carries the spirit's short name first
(`Vengeance: G2 …`).

Result tokens: `+T +2E` pieces added; `Bl` Blight added, `Bl~` Blight that
cascaded in; `Pr†` presence destroyed; `D†×2` Dahan lost; `E† T† C†` Invaders
destroyed; `dmg4` damage dealt; `Df3` Defend; `⇒n` pieces pushed out, `⇐n`
gathered in, `→C2` where they went; `F+2` Fear; `×` a canceled step (`×Di` Disease
stopped the Build, `×SLSF` a Power skipped it); `+Di` token added.

Reveals are written where they happen: the Invader card at `X`, the Event at `Ev`,
Fear cards at `Fr`, the offered Power cards at the gain.

## 3. Header and result

```
[Game "8584090885044354762"]
[Source "EndGame log + save"]
[Spirits "Vengeance as a Burning Plague"]
[Boards "C"]
[Adversary "The Kingdom of England" 4]
[Scenario "-"]
[Result "L Blight T6"]
```

Result: `W Invaders`, `W Terror`, `L Blight`, `L Adversary`, `L Deck`, `L Presence`,
with the turn.

## 4. Annotations (hindsight)

Chess glyphs, same meaning: `!` a strong move, `?` a mistake, `!?` a gamble that
paid, `?!` a gamble that failed. Each glyph carries its line in parentheses and its
confidence in braces, per the hindsight spec section 2b:

```
  F  SLSF>C5[F+1]?  (hold SLSF, FV>C8 {71%: 2 Energy kept; loses only if Jungle2 explores both Jungles})
```

A `{…}` comment is free text. A `(…)` variation is a legal line in this notation
and can be fed back to the simulator.

## 5. Two layers

The **display** form above is for reading and for the hindsight report. The
**canonical** form uses the engine's identifiers instead of short codes
(`StrikeLowWithSuddenFevers>C5`, `VengeanceAsABurningPlagueCardPlays4@C8`) and one
token per line, so it round-trips without a legend; it is what the simulator
parses. The emitter writes both; a parser for the canonical form comes with the
simulate mode.

## 6. Order of work

1. `si notate <game> [--canonical]` from the record (done with this spec).
2. Rei's openings written in SIN as the fixture source, replacing the Rust
   constructors in `tests/rei_openings.rs`.
3. Parser for the canonical form → decisions for `si-rules` simulate mode.
4. Hindsight variations and glyphs emitted by `si hindsight`.
