2. The Ledger (evening): Brett said "let's start with the ledger… restart all the window we're building… come up with great visualizations along the way". Brett: "I guess. It shows basically the info we already know of the game… But it's a start. Add visualizations, graphs, things that could help statistics and the explanation of things we want to convey around the ledger and eventually the ramp and acceleration curves / comparison of how fast we grow vs the opponent."
