## Post-game

Companion review (`si-steam-dump review`): Blight 6 from Ravage, 4 from cascade (one mislabeled, R8); Invaders added 26 (Explore 17, Build 9), removed 20 (Events 5, counterattacks 5, Slow 6, Fear 2, Fast 2); pressure by turn 21, 25, 20, 24, 27, 25; largest jump Turn 4 (+4 from Explore); B7's Turn 2 Blight began as a lone Explorer.

Where it turned (Claude's read, from the log):
1. The solo healthy Blight card holds 3; Turn 2's two Blights (B2 9 damage, B7) left 1, so every turn from Turn 3 was a flip, draw or loss threat.
2. B6 became the unstoppable Ravage: on Turn 4, Beasts Chase Off Homesteaders, Mantle of Dread and Wounded Lands all moved pieces into it (4 Explorers, 3 Towns, 1 City on a blighted land), and the Sands+Wetland card Ravaged it on Turn 6.
3. Fast protection thinned out: Drift (only Fast Defend) Forgotten Turn 3, Concealing Shadows Turn 5; on Turn 6 the hand had no Fast card and Energy went to Slow cards. The saves that worked were Fast effects or Fear cards (Mantle T1, Drift T2, Canny Defense T3, Overseas Trade + Sense of Dread T4, Seek Safety T5).
4. Transformative Sacrifice traded 3 Presence for three Minors, leaving 4–5 Presence while Memory Fades to Dust charged a Power or Presence each turn.
